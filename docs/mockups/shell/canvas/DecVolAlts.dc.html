Reaching Sound from a standing volume slider

When brightness and volume stand in the grid (an option, not the default), the button that opens Sound needs a place that is not on the track, where a drag near the top hit the old round chevron. Six ways, at phone size. Every one keeps a long press on the slider as a shortcut, and a screen reader names the action. Alimardon chose E on 2026-10-09: no button on a standing slider; a right click on a laptop, a still hold on touch.

E. Hold it, or right clickdecided
No button. On touch, a still hold of half a second opens Sound; on a laptop, a right click. The slider only follows a moving finger: a tap never jumps it, and it starts once the finger moves 8 px, so a hold and a drag can never be mistaken for each other.
+ The cleanest look; the whole track is the slider+ The same for every slider: brightness opens Displays+ A tip says "Hold for Sound" the first time; the menu key and the screen reader reach it too- Hidden until the tip or edit mode shows it
