Tray: the arrow, as on Windows, a step further

Your pick: one small arrow beside the tray. The apps you want at hand stay in the panel; the rest wait behind the arrow, one click away. Beyond Windows: an app behind the arrow that has news puts a dot on the arrow, the flyout follows the panel (up from a bottom panel, down from a top bar), it takes the keyboard (Super+B, then the arrows and Enter), and dragging an icon between the flyout and the panel is the whole setting.
