One sign for "more inside": the chevron.

A pill (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, the network) has two parts: its round icon turns it on or off, and the rest, ending in a chevron, opens its list in place. The card slides one level deeper, the same width; the back chevron or Escape returns.

A round toggle opens its details with a right click or a long press, and shows the chevron while the pointer is over it.

The round button beside the volume slider is now the same chevron, not the sliders icon, so it no longer looks like the Settings gear. It opens Sound: the output, each app's volume and the microphone.

Every detail view ends in a link to its Settings page, the one place for everything else.
