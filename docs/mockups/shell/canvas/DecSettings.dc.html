Every setting and its default

The defaults Alimardon took on 2026-10-09, from rounds 5 to 8; the four marked Claude's pick were chosen by the principles and can be overruled. Nothing here is fixed for the person: a row with a key is a setting, and a row marked tokens is drawn from design/tokens.toml, so its colours, sizes and radii change there. Later design rounds may revisit any row. Each setting is a row in Settings, an edel settings key and a line of the settings file, with the same name in all three. The key names are proposals until the step that builds them (M5.9 and M5.31); quick.tiles and notifications.do_not_disturb already exist.
