# The compositor

`edel-compositor`: windows, floating and tiling, title bars, outputs and effects (ADR-002), one of the two long-running processes of the Edel shell. Edition 2024, `rust-version = "1.85"`, GPL-3.0-or-later, `publish = false`. Built on smithay, pinned to `=0.7.0` with only the features in use.

## What belongs here

- Window management, outputs, input routing, title bars, effects and the frame loop. The panel, dock, launcher and notifications belong to shell-ui (M5); settings pages to Settings.
- No extension API, no theme engine, no second renderer (GLES only), no IPC daemon (ADR-002). Floating and tiling are two implementations of `layout::WindowPolicy`, chosen per workspace.
- Colours and sizes come only from `design/tokens.toml` through `tokens::Tokens`; never hard-code one in drawing code.
- Readers of files other releases write (tokens, later `system.toml`) are lenient: unknown keys and bad values keep the built-in value and are reported, never fatal (ADR-008). `tokens::check` is the strict reader for tests and builds.

## Layout

- `lib.rs`: the parts that need no display, tested with `cargo test`:
  - `tokens`: re-exports `edel::tokens` (the design tokens moved to the `edel` library in M5.1b, so shell-ui reads them with the same code).
  - `telemetry.rs`: render times and idle frames; `percentile` is nearest rank, the same rule as CI's desktop test (M4.1); `Summary` prints `frames N, render p50 X ms, p99 Y ms, idle frames Z`.
  - `layout.rs`: the `WindowPolicy` trait (`open`, `close`, `moved`, `arrange`, `rearranges`), `policies`, the table of every policy (one line each), `Workspace` (M5.2g: on each screen, by its name, every policy follows that screen's windows and the active one places them in that screen's area, so windows tile and maximize on their own screen; `switch` and `next` change the policy for every screen; `screen_of` says where a window lies; a floating window `moved` onto another screen moves to it; `arrange(areas)` moves the windows of a screen no longer there to the first), `Floating` (M4.3: centred, cascading `CASCADE` px off a window with the same centre, kept inside the area; the CI pixel checks depend on this rule) and `OutputLayout` (mode, scale snapped to 1/120, logical size). Policies place frames, not windows.
  - `layout.rs` also has `auto_scale` (M4.6a: the EDID rule) and `scale_120` and `snap_scale` (every scale in 1/120 steps from 1 to 4).
  - `cursor.rs` (M4.6b): `arrow`, the pointer's arrow drawn at any size, for when no xcursor theme is installed.
  - `effects.rs` (M5.11a): `Tier` (Lite, Balanced, Full), `starting_tier` (Lite on a software renderer such as llvmpipe, at most Balanced on battery, else Full) and `Deadline`, which drops one tier when 5 of the last 60 frames took over three quarters of the screen's refresh interval, then counts afresh and never raises it within a session; tested.
  - `animation.rs` (M5.11b): `Motion` (`appearance.motion`: full, reduced, off), `style` (how long windows open, close and slide, and the size they grow from, by tier and motion: the "Window animations" default row), `Anim` (eased out progress on the frame clock, never running long when a frame is late), `opening` and `closing` (alpha and size) and `Slide` (an offset from the window's place that shrinks to nothing, started from where the window is drawn when it interrupts another); tested.
  - `tiling.rs` (M4.5): `Tiling`, master and stack with the `gap` token; `tiles` is the geometry, tested for 1 to 6 windows.
  - `desks.rs` (M5.2a): `Desks`, the workspaces: each a `Workspace` of policies and, while hidden, its windows bottom first with their frames; `switch` hides the shown windows and hands back the others, `send` moves a shown window to a hidden workspace's top, placed by its policy there, `set_count` follows the preset (the windows of workspaces that go join the last that stays; never the shown one, which the compositor leaves first); every window keeps its screen (`screen_in` finds it in the areas, else the first; with no screen at all, its frame on its own screen until one comes); `minimize` takes a shown window out of the policies and keeps it on its workspace with its frame, `restore` gives it back to them where it was, `minimized` says where one waits, and `hidden` and `hidden_on` count minimized windows as off screen (M5.2h); tested.
  - `settings.rs` (M4.5): `Settings` (`tiling`, `preset`, `title_bars`, `window_buttons`, `panels`, `outputs`, `motion`, `shortcuts`) from the machine's and the person's system file through `edel::system`, the person's keys winning; `policy` is `shell.tiling` if set, else the preset's (`edel::presets`, M5.1c), and `workspaces` the preset's count (M5.2a); `load` reads both leniently and notes a preset this release lacks.
  - `frame.rs` (M4.4): server-side frames, drawn in screen pixels at the output's scale (M4.6a). `Insets` (the bar above, the border round the rest) turns a window's place into its frame's and back; `hit` says which part of a frame a point is on (title, minimize, maximize, close, an edge or corner within `GRIP` px); `paint` draws a bar described by a `Look` into ARGB pixels; `Text` is the title font (`fontdue`) with its letters cached: `load` takes the tokens' `font.interface` family, finds its file among the system's fonts (`font_file`: `XDG_DATA_DIRS`' `fonts` folders, its variable file, then its regular one) and falls back to the first of `FONTS` that loads.
- `main.rs`: arguments (`--bench`, or `-- PROGRAM ARG...`), the tokens from `/usr/share/edel/design/tokens.toml`, else the built-in ones, and the backend: winit when `WAYLAND_DISPLAY` or `DISPLAY` is set, else DRM.
- `state.rs`: `Edel`, the Wayland state: compositor, shm, xdg-shell (offering maximize only), xdg-decoration, seat, data device, outputs, presentation time, one `Space` with the shown workspace's windows and `desks`, the workspaces (M5.2a); `dirty` says something changed. A new toplevel waits in `unplaced` until its first buffer, then the policy places its frame and it takes the keyboard; `placed`, `outputs_changed` and `state_changed` keep the policy, the space and the state file in step, and a window that resizes itself or changes its title writes the state again; a window that drops its buffer leaves the screen and goes back to `unplaced`, to be placed anew when it draws again. `put` places a frame and tells the window its size and whether it is tiled, unless the window is on a hidden workspace; `relayout` puts every shown window where the active policy says and keeps their stacking order, after a switch (`switch_policy`, for every workspace, and Super+T's `toggle_tiling`, for the shown one), an output change or, in tiling, an open or close. `window_of` finds hidden windows too; one that closes or drops its buffer while hidden leaves its workspace and the state file, and the state file lists the shown workspace's windows, then the hidden ones, each with its `workspace`. `dragging` is set while a `WindowGrab` runs: never ask the pointer `is_grabbed` from code a grab calls, as it waits on its own lock. `listen` opens the socket.
- `extworkspace.rs` (M5.2b): ext-workspace-v1, for the panel's switcher and docks. `Managers` keeps each bound manager with its one group (no capabilities: the preset makes the workspaces), the workspace handles it was sent (user data: the number from 0) and the `wl_output`s the group entered; `announce_workspaces` sends what changed and `done` after a switch, a count change, an output change and each new `wl_output` (`OutputHandler::output_bound`). `activate` is remembered and applied by `switch_workspace` at the manager's `commit`; `stop` gets `finished`.
- `edelshell.rs` (M5.3a): `edel-shell-v1`, our private protocol with shell-ui, generated by `wayland-scanner` from `protocols/edel-shell-v1.xml`, which shell-ui reads too. `Links` keeps each bound link with the policy it was told; `sync_shell`, run by `state_changed`, sends the shown workspace's policy when it changes; `toggle_policy` does what Super+T does; `show_launcher` sends `launcher` to every link (M5.3b) and logs `launcher`; `show_switcher` and `hide_switcher` send the switcher's titles and its end (M5.3c). The interface grows within version 1 until the first public release, as both ends ship together.
- `switcher.rs` (M5.3c): the window switcher, the compositor's own, so it works without shell-ui. `switcher_step` opens it on the window after the focused one, in the order of last use (the stacking order, top first, then the shown workspace's minimized windows), or steps it, back with Shift, sends at most ten titles around the chosen one and logs `switcher at T`; `switcher_done` switches to the chosen window (restoring it if minimized) when the keys' modifiers are let go, logging `switched to window T`, or not on Escape; `switcher_forget` drops a window that closed. `input.rs` forwards the modifiers' release to the app and finishes after it.
- `toplevels.rs` (M5.2d): wlr-foreign-toplevel-management, version 3, for the panel's window list and docks. `Toplevels`, in a `RefCell` on `Edel` as `state_changed` takes `&self`, keeps each bound manager and the handles it was given with what each was last told (`Seen`: title, app id, the client's `wl_output`s the window overlaps, or a minimized one's frame did, maximized, minimized and activated); `sync_toplevels`, run by `state_changed`, `focus` and `output_bound`, gives new windows a handle, sends what changed and `done`, and `closed` for windows gone. a new handle always gets its first `done`; a window minimized on the shown workspace whose screen went is on the screen it would come back on; `activate` brings a minimized window back, else shows a hidden window's workspace first, then focuses it; `close`, `set_maximized`, `unset_maximized`, `set_minimized` and `unset_minimized` act. Windows on hidden workspaces are on no screen.
- `workspaces.rs` (M5.2a): `switch_workspace` (Super+N) unmaps the shown windows into `desks` and maps the other workspace's back, stacked as they were, then lays them out, gives the keyboard to the top one (or nothing) and lets the pointer's focus follow (`repoint`); `move_to_workspace` (Super+Shift+N) sends the focused window, a maximized one at the size it goes back to; `set_workspace_count` follows the preset. A hidden window is deactivated; nothing happens during a drag. Logs `workspace N` and `window T to workspace N`. A switch slides (M5.2f): `snapshot_leaving` keeps each shown window's last picture sliding `desks::slide_by` off the screen, and the other workspace's windows are mapped a screen's width away so the relayout slides them in; on Lite slides take no time. `minimize` (the bar's button, xdg-shell's request, the window list) hides a shown window as a closed one fades, the others filling its place in tiling, and logs `minimized window T`; `restore` shows its workspace, maps it back on top with the keyboard and logs `restored window T`. The state file says `minimized` for each window.
- `outputs.rs` (M4.6a): each output's automatic scale (`set_auto_scale`, from the EDID in `drm.rs`), `Edel::apply_scales`, which gives every output its `outputs.NAME.scale` or automatic one, and `send_scale`, which tells a window's surfaces the scale (`preferred_buffer_scale` and `wp_fractional_scale_v1`; `wp_viewporter` lets them draw at it).
- `pointer.rs` (M4.6b): `Cursors`, what the window under the pointer asked for (a surface, or a shape name from `wp_cursor_shape_v1`), images from the xcursor theme (`XCURSOR_THEME`, else `default`) or the drawn arrow, at the output's scale, and frame callbacks for cursor surfaces. The cursor is a `Kind::Cursor` element, so the DRM compositor puts it on the cursor plane; `EDEL_SOFTWARE_CURSOR=1` draws it into the frame.
- `shellui.rs` (M5.1b): starts `edel-shell-ui` once the desktop is on screen, unless the session runs one program (the greeter), with the session's Wayland socket; a thread waits for it, and when it ends the compositor logs it and starts it again after 500 ms, giving up after 5 runs in a row shorter than 10 s; an image without it is said once. `restart` (M5.4a) ends it with SIGTERM and starts it again at once, counting no failure, when the effective preset changes (`Settings::preset_differs`) or `shell.panels` does (`panels_differ`, M5.4e), logging `restarting edel-shell-ui: WHY`.
- `shortcuts.rs` (M5.13a): `[shortcuts]` from both system files (merged in `settings.rs`, the person's over the machine's) laid over the defaults by `edel::shortcuts::resolve` and turned into keysyms: `bind` gives the bindings and notes, `find` the action for a key press (the Latin layout's keysym and exactly these modifiers), `changes` the `shortcut ACTION is KEYS` lines `reload_settings` logs. Close, tiling, terminal, `workspace_N` and `move_to_workspace_N` act (`program::open` starts foot with the session's socket and display); the `switcher` keys (Alt+Tab) step the switcher, with Shift back (`SwitcherBack`), while `holds_switcher` says its modifiers are held (M5.3c); the launcher's `Super` is a tap (M5.3b): a binding of one modifier alone, which `tapped` matches when that modifier is let go with no other key or button pressed since it went down (`Edel::tap`, and the seat's count of keys down), and the app still hears both; other actions bind nothing until their steps land, so their keys reach the app.
- `watch.rs` (M4.5): reads the settings at start and follows both files with inotify on the event loop (their directories, as writers rename); a change calls `Edel::reload_settings`, which follows the preset's workspace count, switches every workspace when the policy (`shell.tiling` or the preset's) changed, lays the windows out again when `shell.title_bars` did and restyles the animations for `appearance.motion`.
- `decoration.rs` (M4.4): xdg-decoration (a window that asks for our bar or leaves the choice gets it, one that asks to draw its own keeps it), each window's `FrameData` in its user data (the bar's texture, redrawn only when its `Look` changes, the border buffers, the frame to go back to after maximizing), maximize and unmaximize, close, and `load_text`, which loads the font on a thread and redraws the bars when it arrives.
- `render.rs` (M4.4): `Element`, `Drawn` (an element as it is, or grown or shrunk about a window's centre) and `Edel::elements`, what both backends draw: the cursor, the top and overlay layers, the windows from the top, each with its popups, bar and border, faded, grown and moved as `animate.rs` says, closed windows' pictures at the depth they had, then the bottom and background layers.
- `animate.rs` (M5.11b): `Animations`, what is opening, sliding and closing, on a clock started with the compositor. `put` and maximizing call `Edel::slide`, which starts a slide when the window is already on screen elsewhere (a window still opening grows in where it ends up instead, and `unmaximize_under` stops the slide, as a person's drag follows the pointer); the first map calls `opened`; `remember_picture` keeps each drawn window's surface textures, so `snapshot_closing`, called when a toplevel is destroyed, can fade out a client that already exited, and `snapshot_leaving` slides a copy of it off the screen when its workspace is left (`Closing::leave`); `restyle` follows the tier and `appearance.motion` and logs `animations at tier T, motion M: open X ms, close Y ms, slide Z ms` when they change. While `running`, the DRM backend arms a timer for one refresh interval that marks the screens dirty only if no frame was drawn meanwhile (`last_frame`), so animations step with the display, never faster, and add no frame of their own while clients' frames already step them, which on a virtual GPU that shows a frame the moment it is queued would make the next client frame wait; winit asks for another redraw.
- `tiers.rs` (M5.11a): the session's tier: `start_effects` reads the GL renderer's name once the renderer exists (`renderer_name`), the battery from `/sys/class/power_supply`, or `EDEL_EFFECTS=lite|balanced|full`, logs `tier=NAME (why)` and writes `tier` to the state file; `frame_drawn` feeds each drawn frame and the screen's refresh interval to the `Deadline` and logs a drop, after which `restyle` shortens the animations.
- `layers.rs` (M5.1a): `zwlr_layer_shell_v1`, for shell-ui's panels and anything else that draws one. Each screen's layer surfaces live in smithay's `layer_map_for_output`, placed by `LayerMap::arrange` from their anchors and margins; a commit arranges the screen and sends the first configure; `window_areas` gives each screen less its exclusive zones, by name, where windows open, tile and maximize (`output_area` stays the first whole screen, for absolute pointers), and a change to any lays the windows out again; a new window opens on `pointer_screen`, a maximized one fills its `home`, and `screen_at` says where a dragged window's middle landed, a window whose middle is off every screen staying on its own (M5.2g); a layer above the windows that asks for the keyboard alone (the launcher) gets it when it first shows (`give_keyboard`, `keyboard_layer`), gives it back if it hides with a null buffer, and a click on nothing takes it back to the top window (`take_keyboard_back`), as a click on a window does; a window that closes moves the keyboard only if it had it (M5.3b); `under` meets the top and overlay layers before the windows and the bottom and background ones after; a click on a layer that takes the keyboard gives it the keyboard; layers hear their screen's scale and get frame callbacks like windows; the state file lists them as `[[layers]]` (namespace, layer, output, place). A dock (namespace `edel-dock`) hides while a window's frame overlaps it when the effective panels say `hide = "covered"` (`Settings::dock_hides`, M5.4f): `hidden_layers` leaves it out of drawing and of `layer_under`, and `reveal_docks`, on every pointer move, shows it while the pointer is within 2 px of its screen edge or, once shown, over it (`dock_shown`).
- `input.rs`: keyboard, pointer, scrolling and pens (`zwp_tablet_v2`, M4.6b) for both backends (relative and absolute motion, click to focus and raise; `under` finds the frame part or surface at a point; a bar drags, double-clicks to maximize and its buttons act on release; edges resize; Super with the left or right button starts a move or resize; the keys `shortcuts.rs` binds act) and the terminal Ctrl+Alt+F1 to F12 asks for.
- `grabs.rs`: `WindowGrab`, one pointer grab for moving and resizing (`resized` keeps the opposite edges and a 96x64 minimum; a maximized window lets go after 8 px); it hands the final place to `Edel::placed` when the button is released.
- `statefile.rs`: the writer thread of `/run/edel/session/state.toml` (only outside a session it does nothing); the newest state wins. `replace` writes a file whole under `NAME.new` and renames it over the old one, never opening a file through a link; the health file `ready` is written the same way, since the greeter's compositor and the person's replace each other's files (M4.7b).
- `program.rs` (M4.7b): `edel-compositor -- PROGRAM ARG...` starts the program once the first screen has shown a frame (in the development window, from the first turn of the event loop), with `WAYLAND_DISPLAY` and the X11 display as `DISPLAY`; a thread waits for it, and when it exits, or cannot start, the compositor stops. greetd's greeter is `edel-compositor -- foot agreety --cmd 'dbus-run-session -- edel-compositor'`. `open` starts a program in the session the same way, without ending it, as Ctrl+Alt+T does with foot (M5.13a).
- `drm.rs` (M4.2b, M4.6c): the backend for real screens. A libseat session opens the primary GPU (`primary_gpu`, else the first); GBM, EGL and `GlesRenderer`; `Gpu::scan` lights every connected screen the settings want (a `Screen`: its output, a CRTC of its own, a `DrmCompositor`) at `outputs.NAME.mode`, else its preferred mode, else the first it lists, darkens screens unplugged or turned off, and places them with `place_screens`; it runs at start, on udev's change events, when the seat comes back and when `Edel::screens_changed` is set by a change to `[outputs]`; libinput on the session. Windows open on the first screen (`output_area`) until M5.2g; workspaces span every screen. Each screen draws when it is dirty (`Edel::dirty` marks them all) and its last frame's vblank has come; on the vblank, presentation feedback goes to clients, each screen's first frame logs `edel-compositor: output NAME WxH ready`, and the first screen to draw writes `/run/edel/session/ready`, the desktop's health (M4.8). With no screen connected at start the compositor stays up, writes `ready` as `no screen connected yet; waiting for one` and lights the screen udev later reports, so a desktop booted without its screen is never restarted by the boot guard. A timer armed by the first frame after a quiet spell logs the telemetry once the screen has been still for 2 s, so nothing wakes while idle. Pausing the session (another VT) pauses DRM and libinput; activating resets the compositor's state and redraws.
- `xwayland.rs` (M4.7): X11 apps. `listen` takes the first free X11 display (its lock file `/tmp/.XN-lock`, as X servers write it, taken over when its process is gone; its socket in the abstract namespace and in `/tmp/.X11-unix`) and logs `X11 apps on :N`, if `xwayland-satellite` is on the `PATH` (feature `xwayland`). When the first X11 app connects, `xwayland-satellite :N -listenfd A -listenfd B` starts with copies of the sockets (`rustix::io::dup`, so they survive exec), runs XWayland on them, and turns X11 windows into ordinary Wayland windows, so they get our bars, policies and keyboard focus with no X11 code here. While it runs the sockets' sources rest; a thread waits for it, and when it stops, apps still waiting are turned away and the sockets wait again, so the next X11 app starts it anew and a crash never loops. The lock and socket go when the compositor exits. No smithay XWayland feature: smithay 0.7 opens XWayland's sockets itself, so it could not start on demand.
- `winit.rs`: the development backend. A frame is drawn only when `dirty` is set (a commit, a new or closed window, input that focuses, a resize), so an idle desktop draws nothing; `--bench` stops after 5 s and prints the telemetry.

## Running it

On a desktop with X11 or Wayland: `cargo run -p edel-compositor`, then `WAYLAND_DISPLAY=wayland-N foot` with the socket it prints, or `DISPLAY=:N xclock` with the display its `X11 apps on :N` line names, which needs `xwayland-satellite` (Alpine and Arch package it; Ubuntu 24.04 does not, so test X11 apps in an Alpine container or the desktop image). Without a display (a cloud session), `xvfb-run -a cargo run -p edel-compositor -- --bench` works with Xvfb, Mesa (`libegl1`, `libgl1-mesa-dri`) and `libxkbcommon-x11-0` installed; a client such as `weston-simple-shm` makes it draw. The binary links libxkbcommon, libudev, libinput, libgbm and libseat (`libxkbcommon-dev libudev-dev libinput-dev libgbm-dev libseat-dev` on Ubuntu; `eudev-dev libinput-dev libseat-dev libxkbcommon-dev mesa-dev pkgconf` on Alpine); EGL is loaded at run time. In the desktop image greetd starts it; CI's `desktop-test` runs it under llvmpipe with `LIBGL_ALWAYS_SOFTWARE=1 LP_NUM_THREADS=0`, which virtio-vga needs (M4.1).

## Coming changes (roadmap)

`crates/testclient` is `edel-testclient` (M4.3): one window of `--size WxH`, `--colour RRGGBB` and `--title T`, drawn once and again only on resize, for CI's pixel checks; it exits when the compositor goes away. With `--layer top` or `--layer bottom` (M5.1a) it is a panel along that edge instead, on the top layer, its width 0 meaning the screen's and its height its exclusive zone. With `--workspace N` (M5.2b) it draws nothing: it shows workspace N over ext-workspace-v1 and prints the workspaces before and after, the shown one starred (`workspaces 1* 2 3 4`). With `--toplevels [TITLE]` (M5.2d) it lists the windows over wlr-foreign-toplevel-management, the focused one starred and one on no screen marked `-` (`toplevels one* away-`), and given a title activates that window and lists them again once it is focused. Only CI's images ship it.

Make each change only in its step: M5.2g windows on the pointer's screen; M5.10 the compositor greeting people itself.
