# The modules of shell-ui

What each module holds, one bullet per module (moved here from [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) by M6.17, line 11, so the rules a session reads stay short). Read the bullets of the modules you change before editing them, and keep them true in the same PR: a new module adds its bullet, a removed one takes its bullet out, a changed one edits only its own bullet.

- `main.rs`: the Wayland connection; the tokens in the colour scheme `appearance.mode` names, with the person's accent and colours (M5.5c, M5.12b1, `edel::tokens::on_this_machine`); the preset (`layout.preset` from the machine's and the person's settings file through `edel::settings`, else Classic, read once at start with the colour scheme by `from_system_files` through `edel::presets`, its panels replaced by `panels.list` when set, M5.4e) and one layer surface per panel in it, made by `make_panel` (namespace `edel-panel`, along its edge, exclusive zone `size.panel`, a `size.radius` strip on its inner side for the fillets that is neither opaque nor clickable; a dock (M5.4d, `style = "dock"`) is namespace `edel-dock`, anchored to its edge alone 8 px from it, keeps `size.panel` free too unless it hides while covered (`hide = "covered"`, M5.4f, which the compositor does), and asks for a new width from `paint::natural_width` whenever what it holds changes, drawing once the compositor agrees); a change of `panels.list` is followed at once (M5.31b, `settings_changed` in `tray_card.rs` through `edel::panel_edit::applying`): `panels_changed` swaps each panel's widgets and draws again when the panels keep their edges, styles, sizes, floating, screens and hiding, else closes the popups and makes the surfaces anew (`remake_panels`), logging `panels now EDGE (N widgets)`; `make_panels` makes one surface per spec, or on `screens = "every"` one per output (`create_layer_surface` with the output; with none known yet, one the compositor places), and `outputs_changed` (from `new_output` and `output_destroyed`) makes them afresh when a panel is on every screen (M5.31c); a floating bar's surface is anchored to its edge and both sides with margins of `size.gap`, its exclusive zone its height plus the gap; the calloop loop with the minute timer that redraws the panels; buffers from a `SlotPool`; each panel's scale from `preferred_buffer_scale`; the tier from the compositor's state file (fillets unless Lite); a pointer from the seat, whose left clicks and scrolls go to the widget under them (`Shell::input`, by the places `paint` returned; a left click on a kept tray icon acts on release, so the icon can be dragged, M5.9g), and the `Live` state widgets show, kept by `workspaces_changed` and `windows_changed`; scrolls add up in `widgets::Scrolled` (a high-resolution wheel's 120ths, a touchpad's pixels) until they make whole steps; the status area's quick settings (`quick_card.rs`), the notification server's channel on the loop and the banner and the centre (`notify_card.rs`, M5.9b) and the launcher's surface (`Menu`: a `Popup` named `edel-launcher`, anchored beside the first panel's start, 8 px from the panel and the side, with the keyboard alone) and the seat's keyboard (sctk's xkbcommon, with repeat), all made in `open_launcher` and let go in `close_launcher`, which the keyboard leaving also calls; keys go to `launcher_key`, the pointer's motion and clicks on it to its rows; it logs `launcher shown, N apps`, `launched NAME (COMMAND)` and `launcher hidden`; each change of where widgets lie is logged as `panel places NAME X+W, ...` in logical pixels, which CI reads to click them. It exits when the compositor goes away or takes a panel away; the compositor starts it again (`shellui.rs` there). A change of the settings files never starts it again: it follows them (`follow.rs`, M6.17). So the services a widget needs start when a panel first holds it, at start or after a change of the panels (`panels_changed`): `serve_tray` (the tray's watcher) and `watch_status` (the status area's reading and the in-use sign); a session that began on a preset without them, such as Zen, gets them when it moves to one with them. `minute` (M7.7c) is the minute's work (the panels, the launcher's reap, the lock screen and the status reading): `tick` runs it on the clock's minute unless a game plays, and `aside.rs` runs it once when the game ends if a minute came due meanwhile. `saver` (M8.31) is the battery saver as `power_rules::saver` last decided it: `fillets_drawn` draws no fillets while it is on, and the update check waits. `edel-shell-ui --game` is a game session's (M8.26, started so by the compositor): `Shell::game_session` clears the preset's panels at start and makes `panels_changed` and `desktop_follow` (`desktop.rs`) keep none, so the lock screen, the idle ladder, power and notifications run as on the desktop, with nothing drawn over the game.
- `follow.rs` (M6.17): shell-ui follows a change of look while it runs, where the compositor once started it again, which lost the notifications, the tray and anything open: `follow_look`, first in `settings_changed` (`tray_card.rs`), reads `chosen` (the preset's name and the colour scheme, `main.rs`'s `read_choices`), `edel::i18n::follow` and the tokens again, and `why` (pure, tested) gives the reason the compositor gave, first the preset (another than the one in use, a name this release lacks being Classic), the scheme, the tokens (`the text changed`, `the icons changed`, else `the colours changed`; the windows' opacity alone is the compositor's and changes nothing here, as it restarted nothing before), then the language (with none, the name read is kept, so a note is said once); with one it logs `follows the settings: WHY` (and `words in LANG, N translated`, `text FONT at N px` or `icons at N px` when those changed), closes the popups, the panel editor and the lock looks (writing nothing), takes the new tokens (`Text::set_family`, and `place_panels`, which gives each panel whose height, strip or gap changed its new size through `place_panel`, its surface kept), takes the preset's quick tiles, draws every panel again with its places logged again, draws the lock again (`lock_relook`), reads the status again, and opens quick settings again when it was open (its Dark style tile is one way to change the look). The rest of `settings_changed` then follows the panels and tells the portal.
- `paint.rs`: `Look` (everything a panel shows at one scale, what each widget shows included, and `editing`, M5.31b: while the panel is edited each widget gets a tile as wide as its title needs, `EDIT_PAD` 8 px at each side, and each empty group a dashed place, `EMPTY_WIDTH` 40 px; `paint` returns `Places`, the widgets' places and the empty groups', so part 2b can drop there), `Row` (the panel's widgets, from its start, in its centre and towards its end) and `paint`, which draws a panel into a tiny-skia pixmap (its colour, the fillets on either edge and the widgets laid out in their groups; for a dock a card as high as its size's bar plus `DOCK_EXTRA` (20 px, 60 px for medium), rounded all round at 18 px with a hairline of the text's colour at 10 percent inside its edge, 8 px at each end (`DOCK_PAD`), and no fillets; a floating bar (M5.31c, `Look::floating`) is drawn the same way with no strip; `height(style, size, tokens)` is a panel's height by style and size, the tokens `size.panel_small`, `size.panel` and `size.panel_large`) and returns where each widget lies; `natural_width` is how wide a dock is for what it shows, given `(size, scale)`; `strip(style, floating, tokens)` and `panel_top` say where the panel lies in its buffer for each style; `Text` (`edel::text`, shared with the compositor's title bars since M8.21; `paint::text` loads it and says `messages::NO_FONT` when no font loaded) (cosmic-text's fonts and glyph cache, loaded once, in the tokens' `font.interface` family; when fontconfig finds no font it loads `edel::places::FALLBACK_FONTS` itself, and with none of those either it says `messages::NO_FONT` and shapes nothing, as cosmic-text panics shaping with no font and shell-ui draws the lock screen, M6.17) shapes and draws a `Line`, in a `Face` (a weight, regular, medium or semibold, and tabular figures or not, M5.29; `line_in` and `fit_in`, `line` and `fit` being regular), and `fit` cuts one short with an ellipsis; `mix`, `rounded`, `fill`, `colour` and `paint_of` are `edel::draw`'s, re-exported (M6.17: one copy for the compositor and shell-ui); `lit` is the accent at 18%, under a chosen row or a switched-on button; `to_argb` gives `wl_shm`'s byte order. `glass` is the frosted glass every glass surface shares, the tokens' `glass` edged with `glass_rim` (the lock screen's cards, its field and the player), and `rim` edges every pop-up card drawn by `popup::card` and `popup::cards` with two hairlines, the tokens' `edge` outside (which reads on the light scheme) and `glass_rim` inside it (which reads on the dark one) (M8.21 part 21n). Its bar and dock fills are the panel colour at `appearance.panel_opacity` percent (M5.12b2b), the edge and the widgets are not faded; under a see-through fill the glass (M5.12b3, `glass.rs`) is drawn first, in the fill's own shape, so the blurred wallpaper shows through it. Tested without a display. `shadow` (M5.5e) draws a card's shadow from the tokens through `edel::draw::shadow` (M6.17): two layers cast by one light above, a close sharp one and a far soft one, each a rounded-rect mask blurred by three box blurs (`edel::pictures::box_blur`), in the tokens' `shadow` colour. Part 2b (M5.31b): `Look` gains `lifted` (the widget dragged off this panel, drawn on a layer at 35 percent) and `caret` (a 2 px accent line, a button high, at a logical x, where a dragged widget would land). `TITLE_BELOW` (16 logical px): a widget narrower than it shows its title in edit mode, as an empty widget does; `edit_width` (`max(natural, title + 2 * EDIT_PAD)`) is what `paint` and `natural_width` both measure with, and `natural_width` takes `editing`, counting each empty group's `EMPTY_WIDTH` as the dock's tiles need.
- `glass.rs` (M5.12b3, ADR-002's glass decision): the glass behind see-through panels, and the one copy of the blur the lock screen's backgrounds use too (ADR-010). The glass keeps Appearance's wallpaper (`appearance.wallpaper`), not a workspace's own (M5.12b5): the panel follows the workspace's in M8.21. `Glass::get` makes the whole screen's glass once for a wallpaper, a size and `appearance.blur` (0 is the wallpaper sharp; else it is made at a quarter of the size, blurred with `radius`, 0.24 px per percent, and drawn up again), keeps it until one of them changes and logs `glass blur B on WxH`; `under` draws it into a buffer inside a path, moved by the buffer's place on the screen. `solid`, `sharp`, `blurred` and `behind` (the key a wallpaper is kept under) are the lock's too. `main.rs`'s `show` gives `paint` the main screen's glass while `appearance.panel_opacity` is below 100, and `settings_changed` (`tray_card.rs`) draws the panels again when the wallpaper the files name changes; the bar is opaque only at 100 percent. Tested; `EDEL_GLASS_PNG=DIR cargo test -p edel-shell-ui glass_png` writes `panel-glass.png` and `panel-sharp.png`.
- `widgets/mod.rs` (M5.1c): the widget table. A widget is one module here and one line in `TABLE`: its name and its `title` (what Settings, the editor's drawer and a screen reader call it) come from its entry in `edel::widgets` (M5.33b), and the catalogue test holds every surface a widget fits to a drawing module (the panel's line, the lock's `Part`, and on the desktop `desktop.rs`'s `draws`, M8.21 21j1). Its other parts: the feature it needs if any, and its functions: what it shows now, from `Live` (the panel redraws only when that changes), its width and how it draws, on a `Canvas`, both from what it shows, and what an `Input` (a click with the widget's width, a scroll) on it does, given a canvas to measure on as `width` has (M5.29), an `Action` (show a workspace, move the switcher's view, activate or minimize a window) or nothing (`no_input`); and its `role` and `label` for screen readers (M5.1d), with `parts` (`no_parts` for most) when a screen reader should read its pieces one by one, as the tray's icons (M5.2e). `usable` keeps the widgets a preset names that this machine can show and notes the rest: a name the table lacks, or a widget whose feature has no `/usr/share/edel/features/NAME.toml`. The tests fail when a line needs a feature with no file under `features/` or a built-in preset names a widget the table lacks.
- `a11y.rs` (M5.1d): what screen readers read. `tree` makes each panel a window in an AccessKit tree whose children are its widgets of some width, with the role and label from each widget's line (a label's text is its value) and their places from `paint`; a `Reader` per panel holds an `accesskit_unix::Adapter`, which serves the tree over AT-SPI on the session's D-Bus only once a reader turns accessibility on (`org.a11y.Status`). Until then `update` keeps the panel's widgets and builds no tree. A panel's widgets take no actions from a reader yet; a window made with `Reader::pressing` does: its buttons offer a click (`Action::Click`), and each press sends the button's place among the window's items (`top_level`) over a calloop channel to the window's own code, as the Undo bar's does (the whole-system review of 2026-10-25). A widget's `parts` (M5.2e) become the children of its node. The open quick settings card (M5.9a) has a `Reader` of its own (`named`, `update_focused`): a window "Quick settings" whose children are the tiles' switches (`Item::toggled`), their arrows, the volume's slider (`Item::value`), the chevron that opens the outputs, the outputs and Sound settings while open, and Settings, the one holding the keyboard focused. The launcher (M6.9) and the layout styles menu have one each too, "Launcher" (a search field whose typed text is its value, `Item::value_text`, and a list item for each app shown, the chosen one focused) and "Layout styles" (a radio button for each style, on for the one in use). `ci/desktop/features/desktop-test/usr/libexec/edel-a11y-tree` is the test's screen reader. Tested. M8.21 21k2: `Item.disabled` says an item cannot be used now, announced as disabled (`set_disabled`), for a colour swatch that does not read.
- `tray.rs` (M5.2e): the tray's D-Bus side, on the connection and zbus thread of the portal, with no thread of its own. `serve` puts `org.kde.StatusNotifierWatcher` at `/StatusNotifierWatcher` there (`RegisterStatusNotifierItem`, `RegisterStatusNotifierHost` doing nothing, `RegisteredStatusNotifierItems`, `IsStatusNotifierHostRegistered` always true, `ProtocolVersion` 0 and the three signals) and claims the name; if another tray host has it, it says so (`messages::tray_not_served`) and there is no tray. An item is known by its id, `service/path` (`id_of`: a bus name alone is at `/StatusNotifierItem`, an object path is on the sender, both are as given; `parse_id` splits it). Each registered item gets a task on zbus's executor (`watch`): it listens to the item's signals and to its bus name losing its owner (`NameOwnerChanged`), reads `IconName`, `IconPixmap`, `Title` and `ToolTip` fresh (no property cache) at the start and after any signal, and sends `Event::Item` or, when the app leaves the bus, `Event::Gone`, over a calloop channel to the event loop, where `Shell::tray_changed` keeps `Live::tray` and logs `tray: N items` when their number changes. The pixmap (ARGB32 big-endian, the best size by `best_pixmap`, premultiplied by `premultiplied`, shrunk to at most 44 px by `shrunk`) travels as the text `encode` writes, so `shows` stays text. `call` asks an item to `Activate` or `ContextMenu` on the same executor without waiting. The test `an_item_registers_shows_up_and_leaves_with_its_app` runs only with a session bus (`dbus-run-session -- cargo test -p edel-shell-ui tray`) and passes without one. `Id` and `Status` are read as `app` (the `Id`, else the title, which `panels.tray` names) and `attention` (`NeedsAttention`, the dot of M5.9g). Menus over `com.canonical.dbusmenu`, `IconThemePath` and attention and overlay icons are not read yet.
- `widgets/tray.rs` (M5.2e, M5.9g): the tray widget, before the clock in every built-in preset. `split` gives the items kept in the panel, in `Live::tray_in_panel`'s order (the `panels.tray` key, read when the tray changes), and the rest behind the arrow, in the order they came; while any item is behind it the row's first cell is the arrow (`arrow\tHIDDEN\tOPEN\tDOT` as its first line of `shows`: its count, `Live::tray_open` for its lit button, and a dot when a hidden item has attention, a 6 px circle in `title_close_hover` in the cell's top right corner), a chevron pointing down from a panel along the top (`Canvas::along_top`), up otherwise. It shows a line per kept item, `ID\tTITLE\tICON NAME\tPIXMAP`; a 30 px cell each between 2 px margins, the icon 22 px: the item's `IconName` from the icon themes as apps' are, else its pixmap fitted into the square, else the first letter of its title on an accent tile (`draw_icon`, shared with `trayview.rs`). `logical_width` takes the kept count and whether there is an arrow. A click on the arrow is `Action::TrayOpen` (a right click does nothing there), a click on a kept item `Action::Tray(ID, false)` and a right click `Action::Tray(ID, true)`, which `Shell::tray_call` turns into `Activate` or `ContextMenu` with the click's place along the panel (the screen's place is not known to shell-ui). A screen reader hears a group, "Tray: Network, Volume, 2 hidden", whose parts are the arrow ("Hidden icons (2)") and one button for each kept item named by its title (`a11y::Item::children`). Tested.
- `tooltip.rs` (M5.9h, the fifth round's `tray.jpg`): the tray's tooltip, "Hidden icons (6)" in a small label over the arrow, shown after the pointer has rested on it for `DELAY_MS` (600 ms) and gone when it moves off, presses or the grid opens. Plain data and drawing (`View`, `size` from `size.panel_text_small` and `paint` in the panel's and text's colours with `radius_small` corners), tested without a display; `impl Shell` starts its timer from the panel's pointer handling (`tooltip_hover`, `arrow_at` through `widgets::tray::behind_arrow`) and makes its surface, `edel-tooltip`, with no keyboard and no clicks (`Popup::set_no_input`), 6 px from the panel and centred on the arrow (`show_tooltip`, `draw_tooltip`, `hide_tooltip`). Any widget can show one later through this module. Logs `tooltip shown, TEXT` and `tooltip hidden`.
- `trayview.rs` (M5.9g, the fifth round's `tray.jpg`): the tray's grid, plain data and drawing: the apps behind the arrow on one card, four across (three on Compact at touch size, no footer), with a footer hairline, the hint "Drag to the panel to keep" and a gear to the tray's settings. `layout` is the one function that places everything (`Icon`, `View`, `Part`, `Layout`), `hit`, `inside`, `ring`, `key` (`Key`, `Step`, `Act`), `places` (the log line CI reads), `paint` and `nodes`. Tested, with PNGs when `EDEL_TRAY_PNG` is set.
- `tray_card.rs` (M5.9g): the grid's surface, `edel-tray`, opened by the tray's arrow and hung on it (`toggle_tray_grid`, its margins as the styles' menu's, the keyboard exclusive, `tray_key`). Its drag works both ways: an icon dragged off the grid onto the panel's side of it is kept (`keep_in_panel`), and a kept icon dragged well off the panel goes behind the arrow (`tray_panel_release`; a press that did not move is a click). Both write `panels.tray` with `edel::settings::write`, the list's rule being `edel::settings::tray_list_with` and `tray_value` (Settings' Tray card follows the same rule, M5.9h), and what applies without the person's file is taken out, as writers never write a default. `settings_changed` (M5.9h) reads the key again when the compositor says the settings files changed, so Settings' card or `edel settings set` moves an icon at once; the shell's own writes come back through it and change nothing more. A change in what lies behind the arrow logs `tray: N behind the arrow` and closes an open grid whose icons changed, since the arrow it hangs on moves with the panel's width. `tray_key_pressed` is the tray's key (Super+Shift+B, `shortcuts.show_tray`, M5.9h): it opens the grid with the keyboard on its first app, or closes it, and with nothing behind the arrow logs `tray key, but no app waits behind the arrow`. Its logs CI reads: `tray grid shown, N icons`, `tray grid places` (`card WxH`, the icons' places, `bottom` or `top` from the screen's edge, and `left`), `tray grid hidden` and `tray grid opened from the keyboard`.
- `panel_menu.rs` (M5.31b, M5.31c): the panel's right-click menu, plain data and drawing. `rows(facts)` gives the rows for what the panel is (Edit panels; Style and Size, segmented; Floating for a bar or Hide when covered for a dock; On every screen; Move to the other edge; Add there when it is free; Remove with more than one panel), with `label`, `words`, `chosen` and `on`. `layout(view, tokens)` places each row, its control and the hairlines, `hit` finds a row or a segment, `key` says what a key does (`Step`), `change` gives the panels a choice makes through `edel::panel_edit` (`None` when nothing changes), `nodes` is what a screen reader reads, and `places` is the log line CI reads (`card WxH, row edit X+Y+WxH, style bar ...`). `WIDTH` is 300 px. Tested, with no display; the drawing test writes PNGs when `EDEL_EDITOR_PNG` is set. M5.10c6c2: its segmented rows are drawn by `popup::segmented` and laid out by `popup::segments_at`, the one copy the part cards share.
- `panel_menu_card.rs` (M5.31b, M5.31c): the menu's surface, `edel-panel-menu`, hung on the panel at the pointer as the styles' menu is (`open_panel_menu`, which reads the panel's spec through `panel_facts`; a second right click closes it, the keyboard exclusive). `draw_panel_menu` logs `panel menu shown` and `panel menu places` at its first drawing and gives its reader `panel_menu::nodes`. A choice runs Edit panels (closing the menu and calling `open_editor`) or `change_panels`: the new panels go to `save_panels` first, then to `panels_changed`, logging `panel menu changed WHAT, panels.list written` (or `taken out`, or `nothing to write`), and the Undo bar opens over the panel with the old ones (`open_undo_bar`). A refused change logs `messages::panel_not_changed` and the menu stays open. `panel_menu_key` (through `editor_card::key_of`) and `panel_menu_pointer` take its input. `Shell::panel_menu` holds it while open.
- `edit_kit.rs` (M5.10c6c3; M5.10c7g: `slider`, the glass's slider drawn by the Dim of the wallpaper panel, with `TRACK` 4 px, `TRACK_REST` 18 percent and `KNOB` 16 px; only the editor uses it, as `quick::slider` has an icon and a number): the look of every edit mode, plain data and drawing, tested without a display; the lock screen's edit mode, the panel drawer, the Undo bar and the part cards all draw with it, so a change to it changes them all (ADR-010). `Style` (`GLASS` over the lock screen's frosted glass, `DESKTOP` over the desktop's colours, and a `scale` for its corners), `State` (Rest, Hover, Focus, Lifted), `ButtonKind` (Accent, Quiet) and `inks` (the colours a style draws in). `stage` (the screen's deep backdrop with the accent's glow and the picture's shadow), `stage_frame` (the picture scaled into its place, cut to the picture's corners (`picture_corner`, `radius_card` on the glass, `radius_dock` on the desktop), with its hairline), `outline_part` (a part's outline `PART_PAD` outside it: dashed at rest, filled and solid under the pointer or focused with the focus ring, lifted with the accent line and the shadow), `vacate` (the place a part was dragged from), `chip` (the name pill above a part), `guides` (the snap lines, dashed, across the picture only), `sheet` (the drawer's card), `tile` (the raised tile, its icon in the top 60 percent and its title below, a chosen one at half strength) and `button` (the pill) and `hint` (the line at 70 percent). The drawer's shadow is the caller's, because a sheet drawn in a layer of its own size has no room for one. A tile's focus ring lies outside the tile, so the caller draws it on the card. M5.10c7f: `ButtonKind::Light` (the glass text filled, the stage's dark words: Done and Back to editing) and `mark_part`, the lock editor's part marks (`Rest` nothing, `Hover` a faint 25 percent outline, `Focus` the 2 px accent ring). The glass stage darkens towards its foot (`shade_below`: the shadow colour from 45 percent of the height to half strength at the bottom), the picture drawn over it untouched. M5.10c7f colours: the glass stage is `lock_edit_deep` with one `lock_edit_glow` glow (`stage`, the picture's corners in `picture_corner`), the picture's hairline is white at 12 percent, `side_panel` is the wallpaper and docked panels' look (white at 5 percent, a hairline at 9, no shadow), and `mark_part`'s `Focus` is a 1.5 px white ring with a 4 px accent halo outside it. M8.21 21k2: `swatch` draws one colour swatch of a card's grid in Settings' look (`colours.rs`'s paint): a 20 px circle filled with its colour, or with no fill a 2 px ring in the text's ink, the chosen one with a 28 px ring, and drawn at 35 percent when it does not read.
- `editor.rs` (M5.31b, the fifth round's cards, drawn by the same rules as `trayview.rs`; M5.10c6c3: the lock screen's drawer has tabs, `View::tabs` and `tab` (`Part::Tab`, `Step::Tab`, `tabN NAME` in `places`), its tiles may carry a `picture` (`Picture`, shared by `Rc`, so equal while the same) and a `chosen` ring, and `tallest` keeps one height on every tab; M5.10c7a: `heading`, the lock screen's look's title in place of "Edit lock screen", and `subtitle`, the hint in place of the kind's, which a tile dragged over the drawer still replaces): Edit panels' drawer, plain data and drawing. `View` (`Tile`s: each widget's name, its title, what it shows and whether a panel holds it, dimmed at half strength; `compact`, `width`, `hover`, `focus`), `Part` (`Tile`, `Undo`, `Done`), `layout` (the one function that places everything: a card at most `WIDTH` (560 px) wide, or the screen less 32 px, or the screen's width on a Compact screen with three tiles across; `TILE` 112 by 64 px; a hairline and a 44 px footer with Undo and Done, touch-sized on Compact), `hit`, `ring`, `key` (`Act`: Undo, Done or Close; `Step`) and `places` (the log line CI reads), `paint` (each tile a raised rounded tile with the widget drawn in its top part as it is on a panel, its title under it; the hint wrapped to two lines), and `nodes` (a button per tile, "Add TITLE" or "TITLE, on a panel", then Undo and Done). Tested, with PNGs when `EDEL_EDITOR_PNG` is set. Part 2b (M5.31b): `landing` (pure: where a widget dragged to `x` on a panel lands, the group, the widget it lands before and the caret's place, tested), `View` gains `lifted` (a drawer tile being dragged, at 35 percent) and `taking` (a panel's widget is over the drawer: a 2 px accent outline, the hint "Drop here to take it off the panel"), and each tile's picture is drawn whole at its natural width and scaled down to fit the tile less `EDIT_PAD` at each side, never up (`picture`).
