# shell-ui

`edel-shell-ui`: the Edel shell's panels and menus (ADR-002), the second of its two long-running processes. Edition 2024, `rust-version = "1.85"`, GPL-3.0-or-later, `publish = false`.

## What belongs here

- What people use to find and switch things: the panel (M5.1b), its workspace switcher (M5.2c), window list (M5.2h), layout toggle (M5.3a), apps and search field (M5.4c), the launcher (M5.3b) and the window switcher's list (M5.3c), the tray (M5.2e), the status area and quick settings (M5.9a), notifications with the calendar and do not disturb (M5.9b) and the lock screen and greeter (M5.10). Windows, title bars and effects belong to the compositor; settings pages to Settings.
- It draws its own surfaces (ADR-002's shell-ui toolkit decision of 2026-10-03): layer-shell surfaces through smithay-client-toolkit, drawing with tiny-skia into shared memory, text shaped by cosmic-text. No GTK, no GLib, no scripting, no theme engine. [docs/REVIEW-shells.md](../../docs/REVIEW-shells.md) is what its look and motion learn from.
- Every surface works at every size class (M5.6c) and in touch mode: a panel, dock, launcher or switcher drawn on a Compact screen (below 600 logical px) fits it and gives touch targets of at least 44 px, its layout taken from the preset's lines, never from a fixed width (Alimardon, 2026-10-08: phones later, so nothing built now needs rework, M9; the Tablet and Phone presets bring their own panels).
- Colours and sizes come only from the design tokens (`edel::tokens`); never hard-code one in drawing code. The panel's own (M5.29, from the mockups): `size.panel` (40, the bar), `panel_control` (30, a button's height), `panel_icon` (20, an app's icon in a button), `panel_glyph` (18, the shell's own icons), `panel_text` (13) and `panel_text_small` (11, the date and the workspaces' digits); the paddings between them are consts in each widget.
- Redraw only what changed, and only when it changed: an idle panel draws nothing between minutes. Draw a surface at most once per frame: after each drawing it waits for the compositor's frame callback, then draws what shows by then, so its shared buffers stay two however fast things change.
- Every word people read, shown or spoken to a screen reader, is marked with `edel::i18n`'s `tr`, `trf` or `n_` (M5.24a), and `po/shell-ui.pot` is written afresh with `EDEL_WRITE_DOCS=1 cargo test -p edel i18n` in the same commit; log lines stay English.
- **One copy of everything (ADR-010, Alimardon, 2026-10-10):** what two surfaces show is one module both call, never a second copy: the notification card (`notice.rs`), the slider (`quick::slider`), the cards and rows (`popup.rs`), and from M5.10c the player (`player.rs`). Before drawing something, look for the module that already draws it; when a second surface needs a part another surface owns, move the part into its own module first, in the same PR. Each part takes its size and colours from the tokens, so people change it in one place and every surface follows.
- **Stands alone (M8.13):** it needs the `edel` library without its `cli` feature, a Wayland compositor with the layer shell, the session bus for the tray, notifications and the settings portal, and the files `edel::places` names. It never depends on the compositor's code, Settings or the test client, and speaks to the compositor only through Wayland protocols, `edel-shell-v1` among them (`ci/seams.sh`).

## Layout

- `main.rs`: the Wayland connection; the tokens in the colour scheme `appearance.mode` names (M5.5c, `edel::tokens::load`); the preset (`layout.preset` from the machine's and the person's settings file through `edel::settings`, else Classic, read once at start with the colour scheme by `from_system_files` through `edel::presets`, its panels replaced by `panels.list` when set, M5.4e) and one layer surface per panel in it, made by `make_panel` (namespace `edel-panel`, along its edge, exclusive zone `size.panel`, a `size.radius` strip on its inner side for the fillets that is neither opaque nor clickable; a dock (M5.4d, `style = "dock"`) is namespace `edel-dock`, anchored to its edge alone 8 px from it, keeps `size.panel` free too unless it hides while covered (`hide = "covered"`, M5.4f, which the compositor does), and asks for a new width from `paint::natural_width` whenever what it holds changes, drawing once the compositor agrees); a change of `panels.list` is followed at once (M5.31b, `settings_changed` in `tray_card.rs` through `edel::panel_edit::applying`): `panels_changed` swaps each panel's widgets and draws again when the panels keep their edges, styles, sizes, floating, screens and hiding, else closes the popups and makes the surfaces anew (`remake_panels`), logging `panels now EDGE (N widgets)`; `make_panels` makes one surface per spec, or on `screens = "every"` one per output (`create_layer_surface` with the output; with none known yet, one the compositor places), and `outputs_changed` (from `new_output` and `output_destroyed`) makes them afresh when a panel is on every screen (M5.31c); a floating bar's surface is anchored to its edge and both sides with margins of `size.gap`, its exclusive zone its height plus the gap; the calloop loop with the minute timer that redraws the panels; buffers from a `SlotPool`; each panel's scale from `preferred_buffer_scale`; the tier from the compositor's state file (fillets unless Lite); a pointer from the seat, whose left clicks and scrolls go to the widget under them (`Shell::input`, by the places `paint` returned; a left click on a kept tray icon acts on release, so the icon can be dragged, M5.9g), and the `Live` state widgets show, kept by `workspaces_changed` and `windows_changed`; scrolls add up in `widgets::Scrolled` (a high-resolution wheel's 120ths, a touchpad's pixels) until they make whole steps; the status area's quick settings (`quick_card.rs`), the notification server's channel on the loop and the banner and the centre (`notify_card.rs`, M5.9b) and the launcher's surface (`Menu`: a `Popup` named `edel-launcher`, anchored beside the first panel's start, 8 px from the panel and the side, with the keyboard alone) and the seat's keyboard (sctk's xkbcommon, with repeat), all made in `open_launcher` and let go in `close_launcher`, which the keyboard leaving also calls; keys go to `launcher_key`, the pointer's motion and clicks on it to its rows; it logs `launcher shown, N apps`, `launched NAME (COMMAND)` and `launcher hidden`; each change of where widgets lie is logged as `panel places NAME X+W, ...` in logical pixels, which CI reads to click them. It exits when the compositor goes away or takes a panel away; the compositor starts it again (`shellui.rs` there).
- `paint.rs`: `Look` (everything a panel shows at one scale, what each widget shows included, and `editing`, M5.31b: while the panel is edited each widget gets a tile as wide as its title needs, `EDIT_PAD` 8 px at each side, and each empty group a dashed place, `EMPTY_WIDTH` 40 px; `paint` returns `Places`, the widgets' places and the empty groups', so part 2b can drop there), `Row` (the panel's widgets, from its start, in its centre and towards its end) and `paint`, which draws a panel into a tiny-skia pixmap (its colour, the fillets on either edge and the widgets laid out in their groups; for a dock a card as high as its size's bar plus `DOCK_EXTRA` (20 px, 60 px for medium), rounded all round at 18 px with a hairline of the text's colour at 10 percent inside its edge, 8 px at each end (`DOCK_PAD`), and no fillets; a floating bar (M5.31c, `Look::floating`) is drawn the same way with no strip; `height(style, size, tokens)` is a panel's height by style and size, the tokens `size.panel_small`, `size.panel` and `size.panel_large`) and returns where each widget lies; `natural_width` is how wide a dock is for what it shows, given `(size, scale)`; `strip(style, floating, tokens)` and `panel_top` say where the panel lies in its buffer for each style; `Text` (cosmic-text's fonts and glyph cache, loaded once, in the tokens' `font.interface` family) shapes and draws a `Line`, in a `Face` (a weight, regular, medium or semibold, and tabular figures or not, M5.29; `line_in` and `fit_in`, `line` and `fit` being regular), and `fit` cuts one short with an ellipsis; `mix` dims a colour towards another; `lit` is the accent at 18%, under a chosen row or a switched-on button; `rounded` is a rounded rectangle path and `fill` fills one; `to_argb` gives `wl_shm`'s byte order. Tested without a display. `shadow` (M5.5e) draws a card's shadow from the tokens: two layers cast by one light above, a close sharp one and a far soft one, each a rounded-rect mask blurred by three box blurs (`box_blur`), in the tokens' `shadow` colour.
- `widgets/mod.rs` (M5.1c): the widget table. A widget is one module here and one line in `TABLE`: its name (what presets write), its `title` (M5.31b, marked with `n_`: what Settings, the editor's drawer and a screen reader call it), the feature it needs if any, and its functions: what it shows now, from `Live` (the panel redraws only when that changes), its width and how it draws, on a `Canvas`, both from what it shows, and what an `Input` (a click with the widget's width, a scroll) on it does, given a canvas to measure on as `width` has (M5.29), an `Action` (show a workspace, move the switcher's view, activate or minimize a window) or nothing (`no_input`); and its `role` and `label` for screen readers (M5.1d), with `parts` (`no_parts` for most) when a screen reader should read its pieces one by one, as the tray's icons (M5.2e). `usable` keeps the widgets a preset names that this machine can show and notes the rest: a name the table lacks, or a widget whose feature has no `/usr/share/edel/features/NAME.toml`. The tests fail when a line needs a feature with no file under `features/` or a built-in preset names a widget the table lacks.
- `a11y.rs` (M5.1d): what screen readers read. `tree` makes each panel a window in an AccessKit tree whose children are its widgets of some width, with the role and label from each widget's line (a label's text is its value) and their places from `paint`; a `Reader` per panel holds an `accesskit_unix::Adapter`, which serves the tree over AT-SPI on the session's D-Bus only once a reader turns accessibility on (`org.a11y.Status`). Until then `update` keeps the panel's widgets and builds no tree. Widgets take no actions from a reader yet. A widget's `parts` (M5.2e) become the children of its node. The open quick settings card (M5.9a) has a `Reader` of its own (`named`, `update_focused`): a window "Quick settings" whose children are the tiles' switches (`Item::toggled`), their arrows, the volume's slider (`Item::value`), the chevron that opens the outputs, the outputs and Sound settings while open, and Settings, the one holding the keyboard focused. `ci/desktop/features/desktop-test/usr/libexec/edel-a11y-tree` is the test's screen reader. Tested.
- `tray.rs` (M5.2e): the tray's D-Bus side, on the connection and zbus thread of the portal, with no thread of its own. `serve` puts `org.kde.StatusNotifierWatcher` at `/StatusNotifierWatcher` there (`RegisterStatusNotifierItem`, `RegisterStatusNotifierHost` doing nothing, `RegisteredStatusNotifierItems`, `IsStatusNotifierHostRegistered` always true, `ProtocolVersion` 0 and the three signals) and claims the name; if another tray host has it, it says so (`messages::tray_not_served`) and there is no tray. An item is known by its id, `service/path` (`id_of`: a bus name alone is at `/StatusNotifierItem`, an object path is on the sender, both are as given; `parse_id` splits it). Each registered item gets a task on zbus's executor (`watch`): it listens to the item's signals and to its bus name losing its owner (`NameOwnerChanged`), reads `IconName`, `IconPixmap`, `Title` and `ToolTip` fresh (no property cache) at the start and after any signal, and sends `Event::Item` or, when the app leaves the bus, `Event::Gone`, over a calloop channel to the event loop, where `Shell::tray_changed` keeps `Live::tray` and logs `tray: N items` when their number changes. The pixmap (ARGB32 big-endian, the best size by `best_pixmap`, premultiplied by `premultiplied`, shrunk to at most 44 px by `shrunk`) travels as the text `encode` writes, so `shows` stays text. `call` asks an item to `Activate` or `ContextMenu` on the same executor without waiting. The test `an_item_registers_shows_up_and_leaves_with_its_app` runs only with a session bus (`dbus-run-session -- cargo test -p edel-shell-ui tray`) and passes without one. `Id` and `Status` are read as `app` (the `Id`, else the title, which `panels.tray` names) and `attention` (`NeedsAttention`, the dot of M5.9g). Menus over `com.canonical.dbusmenu`, `IconThemePath` and attention and overlay icons are not read yet.
- `widgets/tray.rs` (M5.2e, M5.9g): the tray widget, before the clock in every built-in preset. `split` gives the items kept in the panel, in `Live::tray_in_panel`'s order (the `panels.tray` key, read when the tray changes), and the rest behind the arrow, in the order they came; while any item is behind it the row's first cell is the arrow (`arrow\tHIDDEN\tOPEN\tDOT` as its first line of `shows`: its count, `Live::tray_open` for its lit button, and a dot when a hidden item has attention, a 6 px circle in `title_close_hover` in the cell's top right corner), a chevron pointing down from a panel along the top (`Canvas::along_top`), up otherwise. It shows a line per kept item, `ID\tTITLE\tICON NAME\tPIXMAP`; a 30 px cell each between 2 px margins, the icon 22 px: the item's `IconName` from the icon themes as apps' are, else its pixmap fitted into the square, else the first letter of its title on an accent tile (`draw_icon`, shared with `trayview.rs`). `logical_width` takes the kept count and whether there is an arrow. A click on the arrow is `Action::TrayOpen` (a right click does nothing there), a click on a kept item `Action::Tray(ID, false)` and a right click `Action::Tray(ID, true)`, which `Shell::tray_call` turns into `Activate` or `ContextMenu` with the click's place along the panel (the screen's place is not known to shell-ui). A screen reader hears a group, "Tray: Network, Volume, 2 hidden", whose parts are the arrow ("Hidden icons (2)") and one button for each kept item named by its title (`a11y::Item::children`). Tested.
- `tooltip.rs` (M5.9h, the fifth round's `tray.jpg`): the tray's tooltip, "Hidden icons (6)" in a small label over the arrow, shown after the pointer has rested on it for `DELAY_MS` (600 ms) and gone when it moves off, presses or the grid opens. Plain data and drawing (`View`, `size` from `size.panel_text_small` and `paint` in the panel's and text's colours with `radius_small` corners), tested without a display; `impl Shell` starts its timer from the panel's pointer handling (`tooltip_hover`, `arrow_at` through `widgets::tray::behind_arrow`) and makes its surface, `edel-tooltip`, with no keyboard and no clicks (`Popup::set_no_input`), 6 px from the panel and centred on the arrow (`show_tooltip`, `draw_tooltip`, `hide_tooltip`). Any widget can show one later through this module. Logs `tooltip shown, TEXT` and `tooltip hidden`.
- `trayview.rs` (M5.9g, the fifth round's `tray.jpg`): the tray's grid, plain data and drawing: the apps behind the arrow on one card, four across (three on Compact at touch size, no footer), with a footer hairline, the hint "Drag to the panel to keep" and a gear to the tray's settings. `layout` is the one function that places everything (`Icon`, `View`, `Part`, `Layout`), `hit`, `inside`, `ring`, `key` (`Key`, `Step`, `Act`), `places` (the log line CI reads), `paint` and `nodes`. Tested, with PNGs when `EDEL_TRAY_PNG` is set.
- `tray_card.rs` (M5.9g): the grid's surface, `edel-tray`, opened by the tray's arrow and hung on it (`toggle_tray_grid`, its margins as the styles' menu's, the keyboard exclusive, `tray_key`). Its drag works both ways: an icon dragged off the grid onto the panel's side of it is kept (`keep_in_panel`), and a kept icon dragged well off the panel goes behind the arrow (`tray_panel_release`; a press that did not move is a click). Both write `panels.tray` with `edel::settings::write`, the list's rule being `edel::settings::tray_list_with` and `tray_value` (Settings' Tray card follows the same rule, M5.9h), and what applies without the person's file is taken out, as writers never write a default. `settings_changed` (M5.9h) reads the key again when the compositor says the settings files changed, so Settings' card or `edel settings set` moves an icon at once; the shell's own writes come back through it and change nothing more. A change in what lies behind the arrow logs `tray: N behind the arrow` and closes an open grid whose icons changed, since the arrow it hangs on moves with the panel's width. `tray_key_pressed` is the tray's key (Super+B, `shortcuts.show_tray`, M5.9h): it opens the grid with the keyboard on its first app, or closes it, and with nothing behind the arrow logs `tray key, but no app waits behind the arrow`. Its logs CI reads: `tray grid shown, N icons`, `tray grid places` (`card WxH`, the icons' places, `bottom` or `top` from the screen's edge, and `left`), `tray grid hidden` and `tray grid opened from the keyboard`.
- `panel_menu.rs` (M5.31b, M5.31c): the panel's right-click menu, plain data and drawing. `rows(facts)` gives the rows for what the panel is (Edit panels; Style and Size, segmented; Floating for a bar or Hide when covered for a dock; On every screen; Move to the other edge; Add there when it is free; Remove with more than one panel), with `label`, `words`, `chosen` and `on`. `layout(view, tokens)` places each row, its control and the hairlines, `hit` finds a row or a segment, `key` says what a key does (`Step`), `change` gives the panels a choice makes through `edel::panel_edit` (`None` when nothing changes), `nodes` is what a screen reader reads, and `places` is the log line CI reads (`card WxH, row edit X+Y+WxH, style bar ...`). `WIDTH` is 300 px. Tested, with no display; the drawing test writes PNGs when `EDEL_EDITOR_PNG` is set.
- `panel_menu_card.rs` (M5.31b, M5.31c): the menu's surface, `edel-panel-menu`, hung on the panel at the pointer as the styles' menu is (`open_panel_menu`, which reads the panel's spec through `panel_facts`; a second right click closes it, the keyboard exclusive). `draw_panel_menu` logs `panel menu shown` and `panel menu places` at its first drawing and gives its reader `panel_menu::nodes`. A choice runs Edit panels (closing the menu and calling `open_editor`) or `change_panels`: the new panels go to `save_panels` first, then to `panels_changed`, logging `panel menu changed WHAT, panels.list written` (or `taken out`, or `nothing to write`), and the Undo bar opens over the panel with the old ones (`open_undo_bar`). A refused change logs `messages::panel_not_changed` and the menu stays open. `panel_menu_key` (through `editor_card::key_of`) and `panel_menu_pointer` take its input. `Shell::panel_menu` holds it while open.
- `editor.rs` (M5.31b, the fifth round's cards, drawn by the same rules as `trayview.rs`): Edit panels' drawer, plain data and drawing. `View` (`Tile`s: each widget's name, its title, what it shows and whether a panel holds it, dimmed at half strength; `compact`, `width`, `hover`, `focus`), `Part` (`Tile`, `Undo`, `Done`), `layout` (the one function that places everything: a card at most `WIDTH` (560 px) wide, or the screen less 32 px, or the screen's width on a Compact screen with three tiles across; `TILE` 112 by 64 px; a hairline and a 44 px footer with Undo and Done, touch-sized on Compact), `hit`, `ring`, `key` (`Act`: Undo, Done or Close; `Step`) and `places` (the log line CI reads), `paint` (each tile a raised rounded tile with the widget drawn in its top part as it is on a panel, its title under it; the hint wrapped to two lines), and `nodes` (a button per tile, "Add TITLE" or "TITLE, on a panel", then Undo and Done). Tested, with PNGs when `EDEL_EDITOR_PNG` is set. Part 2b (M5.31b): `landing` (pure: where a widget dragged to `x` on a panel lands, the group, the widget it lands before and the caret's place, tested), `View` gains `lifted` (a drawer tile being dragged, at 35 percent) and `taking` (a panel's widget is over the drawer: a 2 px accent outline, the hint "Drop here to take it off the panel"), and each tile's picture is drawn whole at its natural width and scaled down to fit the tile less `EDIT_PAD` at each side, never up (`picture`).
- `editor_card.rs` (M5.31b): the drawer's surface, `edel-editor`, above the bottom panel (below the top one when there is none), the keyboard exclusive. `Editor` keeps the drawer (`popup`, `view`, `keyboard`, `reader`, the last places logged) and the panels it started from (`original`) and what they are now (`now`). `open_editor` builds the tiles from the widgets this machine can show (`widgets::usable`) and marks those a panel holds; the panels then draw their widgets as tiles (`Look::editing` is read from `Shell::editor` at each drawing). `undo_editor` puts the panels back, `close_editor(write)` closes the drawer: with `write` (Done) `save_panels` writes `edel::panel_edit::to_write` as `panels.list` with `edel::settings::write` (or takes the person's own line out when what applies without it is what is shown, else logs `nothing to write`), without it (Escape, the keyboard leaving) the panels go back to `original`. Logs `panel editor shown, N widgets in the drawer` (at its first drawing), `panel editor places`, `panel editor undo`, `panel editor done, panels.list written` (or `taken out`, or `nothing to write`), `panel editor undone` and `panel editor hidden`. A press on a panel does nothing while it is open, until the editor's drags come. Part 2b (M5.31b): a press on a widget's place on a panel, or on an unplaced drawer tile, starts a `Drag` (`start` in screen logical pixels, `moving` once it has gone `editor::DRAG_START`, logged `panel editor drag NAME`, and `over`: `Over::Nothing`, `Over::Panel(index, Landing)` or `Over::Drawer`); the geometry is `screen_size` (the first output's logical size), `panel_rect` (a bar its screen's width, a floating bar `size.gap` in from its edge and both sides, a dock centred `DOCK_MARGIN` from its edge, each as high as its size; the main screen's only, as a panel on every screen is placed by its own output, M5.31c), `drawer_rect` (the surface with its room, centred, `above` from its edge, kept in `Editor` as `edge` and `above`) and `over_at`; `drop_drag` moves (`panel_edit::move_widget`, the index taken as the landing's, less one when it is later in the same group), takes out or adds the widget, logs `panel editor moved|took out|added NAME`, and a refused change says so through `messages::panel_not_changed`. `editor_panel_pointer` takes a panel's events while the editor is open. `save_panels` returns what it did (`panels.list written`, `taken out`, `nothing to write`, or `None` after saying why), and Done opens the Undo bar (`UndoBar`, surface `edel-panels-undo`, `open_undo_bar`, `undo_timer`, `draw_undo_bar`, `undo_panels`, `undo_pointer`) where the drawer was, for `undo_bar::SECONDS` (the time waits while the pointer rests on it): Undo writes the panels as they were before the editor opened, logs `panels undone, WHAT`, and the bar closes (`panels undo bar shown|places|hidden`). `close_popups` closes the bar. It has no keyboard, so the keyboard leaving never reaches it. The panel menu (M5.31c) shares `above_panel` (the height above a panel that the drawer and the Undo bar start at), `save_panels`, `open_undo_bar` and `key_of`, so they are `pub(crate)`.
- `undo_bar.rs` (M5.31b, part 2b): Done's Undo bar, plain data and drawing: "Panels changed" (`TEXT` 128 px, fitted) and the Undo pill as the drawer's (88 px wide, 36 high, 44 on Compact), `layout` (the one function that places it), `hit`, `places` (the log line CI reads, `card WxH, undo X+Y+WxH`), `nodes` (a label and a button), `paint`, and `SECONDS` (10). Tested, with PNGs when `EDEL_EDITOR_PNG` is set.
- `paint.rs` (M5.31b, part 2b): `Look` gains `lifted` (the widget dragged off this panel, drawn on a layer at 35 percent) and `caret` (a 2 px accent line, a button high, at a logical x, where a dragged widget would land). `TITLE_BELOW` (16 logical px): a widget narrower than it shows its title in edit mode, as an empty widget does; `edit_width` (`max(natural, title + 2 * EDIT_PAD)`) is what `paint` and `natural_width` both measure with, and `natural_width` takes `editing`, counting each empty group's `EMPTY_WIDTH` as the dock's tiles need.
- `widgets/menu.rs`: the menu button's icon, `design/icons/menu.svg` (four rounded squares, 15 units across in an 18-unit box, M5.5d), in a tile `size.panel_control` high and a fifth wider (36 by 30 px) after 6 px of room and before 2, the icon `size.panel_glyph` (18 px) in the panel text's colour; while the launcher is open (`Live::launcher`, which `open_launcher` and `close_launcher` set) the tile is filled with the panel text at 8.5 percent and the icon is the accent, as the mockups show it (M5.29); a click opens or closes the launcher (M5.3b).
- `widgets/separator.rs` (M5.29): a thin vertical line, one screen pixel wide, `size.panel_control` times 0.6 high, in the panel text's colour at 15 percent, with 6 px on each side; Classic puts one after the menu button and one before the tray; no label, so a screen reader does not meet it.
- `popup.rs` (M5.3): what the launcher and the switcher share. `Popup` is a surface of its own on the overlay layer with its own `SlotPool`, made when shown and dropped when hidden: `canvas` gives a pixmap only when configured and what it shows changed, `show` attaches it, `resize` asks for a new size. `card` draws the menus' card (the panel's colour, `size.radius` corners) and `rows` a list of labels `size.row` high, the chosen one `lit` with `size.radius_control` corners; `radio` draws a radio button. A change to those tokens reaches both. Since M5.9b it also holds what quick settings, the banner and the notification centre lay out with: `Rect`, `dim`, `veil`, `knob`, `raised` (a surface raised on a card, white on the light scheme), `icon_in` and `wrap` (a text broken into at most N lines at spaces, a word wider than a line broken where it fills one, the last line holding the rest to be cut by `Text::fit`). A popup may hold several cards apart (`Card`, a rect and its corners' radius; `set_cards`, drawn by `cards`): each casts its own shadow and takes clicks, the room between them neither, as the notification centre's two cards and quick settings' rounder one. Tested. On the Full and Balanced tiers a popup has `room` round its card (M5.5e, `paint::shadow_room`, the tokens' `shadow_blur` plus `shadow_offset`, none on Lite): the surface is that much bigger, takes clicks only on the card, the launcher's margin shrinks by it so the card keeps its place, pointer positions are taken from the card's corner, and `show` lays the card over `paint::shadow`, drawn once per card size and scale. Since M5.10c `focus_ring` lives here too: the keyboard's focus, a 2 px accent ring 3 px outside a part, at the part's corner radius.

- `lock_card.rs` (M5.10a): the shell's side of ext-session-lock-v1. `lock_screen` (the `lock` event) closes every popup and asks to lock; `locked` makes a lock surface on every screen (`add_lock_screen`, one more for a screen that comes, `lock_output_new`; `lock_output_gone`) and takes the keyboard (`lock_key`: Return checks, Backspace takes a letter back, Escape empties the field, text is typed; keys while checking are dropped); `draw_lock` draws each configured surface whose view changed, on a key, an answer and the minute, with its own `SlotPool`; `check_password` runs `lock::check` on a thread whose answer comes back over the channel `answers` makes (`password_checked`); a right one unlocks and lets everything go (`unlock_screen`). Logs `lock asked for`, `screen locked, N screens`, `lock: checking a password of N letters`, `lock: wrong password` and `screen unlocked`; failures through `messages.rs` (`lock_not_offered`, `LOCK_REFUSED`, `NO_PASSWORD_CHECKER`, `password_not_checked`). Since M5.10c: Caps Lock comes from the keyboard's modifiers (`lock_caps`, called from main.rs's `update_modifiers`); the count of unread notifications is redrawn when `count_unread` changes it; the pointer (`is_lock`, `lock_pointer`) lights the go button when it is over it, and a click there checks the password as Return does; a wrong password shakes the field on a 16 ms timer (`shake_field`, none unless `appearance.animations` is full, read through `edel::settings::ANIMATIONS`). It logs `lock places field X+Y+WxH, go X+Y+WxH`, with `, count Y` while unread notifications show (`lock::places`, in logical pixels, once per screen size). Since M5.10c the corner and its menu are drawn over each screen and take the pointer; Escape closes the menu first; it logs `power menu shown, 3 rows`, `power menu hidden`, `power: NAME`, and the corner's places (`corner::places`) after the lock's on the `lock places` line. Since M5.10c4 the player card (`player.rs`, `Shape::Wide`, on `lock::glass` with `lock::lift`'s shadow, which is drawn only round a part so the glass stays clear, and the app's icon from `quick_card::player_icon`, shared with quick settings) shows in the bottom left corner (under the clock on a Compact screen) from the first play while locked (`lock_players_changed`, fed by `quick_card.rs`'s `player_changed` and kept by `LockCard` with its own `mpris::Follow`) until unlock; its close button (a disc in `tokens.lock_glass` at the top right corner) hides it while nothing plays, and it redraws each second while something plays (`start_player_timer`). Its places go on the `lock places` line as `player X+Y+WxH, play X+Y+WxH`, and it logs `lock player shown, TITLE by ARTIST` and `lock player hidden`.
- `corner.rs` (M5.10c, `docs/mockups/shell/lock.jpg`): the lock screen's bottom right corner, plain data and drawing: round frosted buttons, Power and, with two or more layouts, the keyboard layout (`BUTTON` 40 px, `GAP` 10, `EDGE` 24 from the screen's edges), and Power's menu above them (`MENU_WIDE` 216, rows `ROW` 40: Suspend, Restart with "Update waiting" while `power::restart_waiting`, and Shut down). `View`, `Hit` (`Button` or `Row`), `layout` (the one function that places the corner), `hit`, `places` (the log line CI reads, `power X+Y+WxH`, `layout` when shown, then `menu` and each row by its choice's name) and `paint`. Tested without a display; the drawing test writes `corner.png` when `EDEL_LOCK_PNG` is set.
- `power.rs` (M5.10c): `Choice` and `CHOICES` (suspend, restart, shut down), each with its label, icon and name and its command, `edel suspend|restart|shutdown`, which run the system's program (`/usr/sbin/zzz`, `/sbin/reboot` or `/sbin/poweroff`) through `doas -n`; the login feature's doas rule lets the seat group run exactly those three with `-n`, so nothing asks for a password. `run` runs one on a thread of its own (`lock_card.rs`) and says what failed through `messages::power_failed`. `restart_waiting` reads `edel::places::RESTART_PENDING`, which `edel update` and `edel rollback` leave and `/run` forgets at the next boot. Tested.
- `greetd.rs` (M5.10b): greetd's IPC, the greeter's side, with no JSON library: each message its length in four bytes of the machine's own order then a flat JSON object; `create_session`, `answer` (`post_auth_message_response`), `start_session` and `CANCEL` written with `quoted`, and `reply` reading `Reply` (success, a prompt, secret or not, an info message, an error, `auth_error` or another) from `fields`, the string fields of an object, other values skipped; `Greetd` (the socket `GREETD_SOCK` names, `ask`), `send` and `receive`; `log_in` answers every secret prompt with the password, a visible one with the user name, an info message with nothing, starts the session with the command on success, and cancels on a refusal or a failure, giving `Login` (started, refused, failed). Tested, against a fake greetd over a socket pair.
- `greeter.rs` (M5.10b): `edel-shell-ui --greeter --cmd COMMAND`, which `main.rs`'s `run` hands over to before anything else: a small client of its own (no panels, no tray, no session bus), a layer surface over everything on every screen (`edel-greeter`, the overlay layer, the keyboard exclusive), drawn by `lock::paint`. Since M5.10c the machine's people (`edel::users::read`, through `people::from_users`) stand in a row (`people::Row`, drawn by `people::paint` while `View::people` is set): the first person is chosen at start and their password asked at once; left and right (`people::step`) choose while the field is empty, and so does a left click on a person's disc (`people::hit`, after the corner and before the go button), each choice logged as `greeter: chose LOGIN` or `greeter: chose other`. Other, at the row's end, keeps two steps: "Who is logging in?" over a field showing the user name as typed (`View::asking_name`), then the person's initial and name over the password field. The corner (`corner.rs`) holds Power only (`layout: None`): a click on it toggles its menu, a click on a row runs its choice through `power.rs` (logs `greeter: power menu shown, 3 rows`, `greeter: power menu hidden` and `greeter: power: NAME`), a press elsewhere with the menu open only closes it, Escape closes it first, and motion and Enter light a button or row (`corner_hover`). Every redraw logs `greeter places PEOPLE, LOCK, CORNER` when it changes (`people::places`, `lock::places`, `corner::places`), the line the desktop test reads to pick ci. Return moves on, or logs in on a thread (`greetd::log_in`, the session `COMMAND` sent as one word, as greetd joins the words with spaces and runs them through `sh` itself, so a list such as `/bin/sh -c COMMAND` would lose its quoting), whose `Login` comes back over a calloop channel: started ends the greeter, and with it its compositor; refused says "Wrong user name or password" and asks again (a chosen person's password at once, Other's user name, which stays typed); failed says "Could not log in, try again" (`messages::login_failed`). Escape goes back a step. Logs `greeter shown, N screens`, `greeter: logging USER in`, `greeter: wrong user name or password` and `greeter: session started for USER`.
- `link.rs` (M5.3a): the client of `edel-shell-v1`, the compositor's private protocol, generated by `wayland-scanner` from `../compositor/protocols/edel-shell-v1.xml`: the policy event into `Live::policy` and `toggle_policy`, the keyboard event into `Live::keyboard` and `next_keyboard_layout` (M5.9f), the compositor's media keys into `Shell::media_key` (`osd_card.rs`), the tray's key (`tray` event, M5.9h) into `Shell::tray_key_pressed`, and the `settings` event (M5.9h, sent at every change of the settings files) into `Shell::settings_changed`, and the `lock` event (M5.10a) into `Shell::lock_screen`.
- `widgets/windows.rs` (M5.2h, M5.29): the window list. It shows a line per window on a screen, `*TITLE` for the focused one, `-TITLE` for a minimized one, ` TITLE` else (each with its icon name before a tab); a button is as wide as its content, 7 px, the app's icon (`size.panel_icon`, 20 px; the generic `app-generic` when the app has none), 8 px, the title in `size.panel_text` at medium weight (`Face::MEDIUM`), 10 px, at most 200 px, 30 px (`size.panel_control`) high with the controls' radius, 2 px between buttons and at each end; together they take at most 45% of the panel, and when their natural widths do not fit the widest are cut back to one common width (`plan`), so a short title keeps its own; when even icons alone (34 px) do not fit, `plan` gives as many as do, the last counting the windows left over (`+N`) and bringing the first of them forward; a button too narrow for 24 px of title shows its icon alone, centred. The focused window's button is lit (the panel text at 6 percent) with a 14 by 2 px accent line, rounded, in the middle of the button at its foot; a running one has a 4 px neutral line, a minimized one dimmer text, a half-faded icon and no mark. Where each button lies depends on the titles' widths in the font, so `Widget::input` is given a canvas to measure on (`main.rs` makes a strip as wide as the panel), and `input`, `width` and `draw` share `plan_for`. A click on the focused window minimizes it, on another activates it. A screen reader hears "Windows: Files (focused), Mail (minimized)". Tested.
- `toplevels.rs` (M5.2h): the wlr-foreign-toplevel-management client, through `Dispatch2` user data (`Manager`, `Handle`): each window's title, screens, focus and minimized state; `tasks` gives the windows on a screen, in order, which `activate` (with the seat) and `minimize` address by place; `windows_changed` at each `done` and `closed`; without the protocol the list shows nothing.
- `workspaces.rs` (M5.2c): the ext-workspace-v1 client, through sctk's `Dispatch2` user data (`Manager`, `Group`, `Handle`): the workspaces by name with the shown one marked, `show(place)` (the workspace at that place in the list: activate, then commit), and `workspaces_changed` at each `done`; a workspace or group removed is destroyed; without the protocol the switcher shows nothing. Each group's screens and workspaces are kept (M5.2o): `names(output)` and `show(output, place)` use the group of the panel's screen (its own, or the one its surface entered, `Panel::entered`), else the first group.
- `workspace_drag.rs` (M5.2p): a left press on a button of the numbers look waits for the release, as `apps_drag.rs`'s does: not moved, it shows the workspace; moved and let go over the same switcher, `widgets::workspaces::landing` gives the place, `link.move_workspace` asks the compositor to move it there, which writes the names in their new order; the caret shows the landing while dragging.
- `widgets/clock.rs` (M5.29): the clock, the time (`HH:MM`, semibold, `size.panel_text`) over a short date (`Sat 3 Oct`, regular, `size.panel_text_small`, dimmer) in tabular figures (`Face::tabular`, Inter's `tnum`), right-aligned with 6 px before and 8 px after, set 1.2 times the time's size apart as the mockups; it shows both lines (`HH:MM\nSat 3 Oct`, English until M5.24) and a screen reader hears "14:05, Sat 3 Oct"; and the time until the next minute. Tested, with fonts: two bands of ink, the date below, dimmer and no taller, and the lit button.
- `widgets/bell.rs` (M5.9k): the notifications button, `notifications`, which no built-in preset holds and any panel can (Edit panels, `panels.list`): the shell's `bell` icon (`size.panel_glyph`) in a square tile `size.panel_control` high with 2 px each side, lit as the menu button's while the notifications it opened are open (`Live::bell_open`). While notifications are unread (`Live::unread`) it shows what `notifications.badge` says (`Live::badge`, read at start and by `settings_changed`, `notification_badge` in `main.rs`): a 7 px dot in the accent ringed in the panel's colour, 2 px out from the bell's top right (`dot`, the default), a pill in the accent with the number in `accent_text` (`count`, `count_text`: at most `99+`), or nothing (`none`). - `widgets/title.rs` (M5.4a): the focused window's title, for bars along the top such as Hive's: the panel text's colour and size, cut short at 40% of the panel, no width while no window has the keyboard. Tested.
- `widgets/apps.rs` (M5.4c): the apps, as a taskbar shows them. It shows a line per cell, `STATE\tID\tICON\tNAME` (`*` focused, `+` running, a space for a pinned app not running): the preset's pinned apps (`Live::pinned`, read once at start, only when a panel holds the widget), then every other app with a window open, named and drawn from `Live::installed` or from its app id. A click is `Action::App(ID)`: `main.rs` minimizes the app's focused window, else brings its first window forward, else starts it through `Launcher::run`. Tested. M5.31d: `cell_side`, `cell_at`, `boundary_x` and `landing` (pure, tested) find the cell under a point and the place a dragged app lands at; the pinned cells come first in `Live::pinned`, one per pin that resolves, in `apps_drag::resolve_pins`' order.
- `apps_drag.rs` (M5.31d): the apps widget's drag. A left press on an app's cell (`press_app`, not while the editor is open) waits for the release, as a kept tray icon does: released without moving (`moved`, the tray's six pixels) it is the click it was (`open_app`); released off the panel (`off_panel`, the tray's rule) a pinned app is unpinned (`unpinned NAME`); released over its own apps widget it is pinned at the landing (`pinned NAME at N`, the place counted after the app is taken out, `panel_edit::pin_at`). `app_caret` puts the panel's `Look::caret` where it would land while the pointer is over that widget, and `drag_marks` reads it in `editor_card.rs`. Each change is written as `panels.pinned` by `panel_edit::pins_to_write` and `edel::settings::write` (`pins_not_kept`, `PINS_NO_HOME`), and `set_pins` makes the new list what the widget shows at once, logging `pinned apps A, B`; the start and `settings_changed` (`tray_card.rs`) read the files the same way (`panel_edit::pins_applying`). - `widgets/search.rs` (M5.4c): a 180 px wide pill, `size.panel_control` high, with a magnifier and "Search" in `size.panel_text`; a click opens or closes the launcher. Tested.
- `shell_bus.rs` (M5.31d): the panel editor on the session bus. `ShellBus` is the object at `SHELL_PATH` with one method, `EditPanels` (`edel::panel_edit`'s names; a test holds the interface's literal to them), which sends `()` over a calloop channel and returns at once; `main.rs` inserts the receiver and each call logs `panel editor asked for over the bus` and opens the editor (`open_editor`, which does nothing while it is open). Served by `portal.rs` on the portal's connection, so it needs the session bus and no thread of its own. Tested.
- `portal.rs` (M5.5a): the settings portal's backend. `serve` (M5.31d: also given the editor's channel) claims `org.freedesktop.impl.portal.desktop.edel` on the session's bus, requests the name `org.edel.Shell` after building (a name another program holds logs `messages::edit_bus_not_served` and leaves the portal served) and serves `org.freedesktop.impl.portal.Settings` at `/org/freedesktop/portal/desktop` on zbus's own thread (the connection lives in `Shell`); `settings` is `org.freedesktop.appearance` from the tokens: `color-scheme` 1 when the background is dark, else 2, `accent-color` the accent, `contrast` 0. Once serving, it emits `SettingChanged` for each (M5.5c), as shell-ui starts again when the colour scheme changes, so apps already open follow. xdg-desktop-portal, which D-Bus starts when an app first asks, passes it on, by `/usr/share/xdg-desktop-portal/portals/edel.portal` and `edel-portals.conf` in the shell feature. Without a session bus it says so and serves nothing. Tested.
- `features/NAME.rs` (none yet): code that talks to an OS feature, such as NetworkManager for `network` (M5.9); its widget's line says `needs: Some("NAME")`.

## Running it

Inside a running compositor: `WAYLAND_DISPLAY=wayland-N cargo run -p edel-shell-ui`. The compositor starts it by itself when `edel-shell-ui` is on the `PATH`, as in the desktop image (the `shell` feature).
