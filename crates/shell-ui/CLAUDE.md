# shell-ui

`edel-shell-ui`: the Edel shell's panels and menus (ADR-002), the second of its two long-running processes. Edition 2024, `rust-version = "1.85"`, GPL-3.0-or-later, `publish = false`.

## What belongs here

- What people use to find and switch things: the panel (M5.1b), its workspace switcher (M5.2c), window list (M5.2h), layout toggle (M5.3a), apps and search field (M5.4c), the launcher (M5.3b) and the window switcher's list (M5.3c), the tray (M5.2e), and later notifications and quick settings (M5.9), the lock screen and greeter (M5.10). Windows, title bars and effects belong to the compositor; settings pages to Settings.
- It draws its own surfaces (ADR-002's shell-ui toolkit decision of 2026-10-03): layer-shell surfaces through smithay-client-toolkit, drawing with tiny-skia into shared memory, text shaped by cosmic-text. No GTK, no GLib, no scripting, no theme engine. [docs/REVIEW-shells.md](../../docs/REVIEW-shells.md) is what its look and motion learn from.
- Every surface works at every size class (M5.6c) and in touch mode: a panel, dock, launcher or switcher drawn on a Compact screen (below 600 logical px) fits it and gives touch targets of at least 44 px, its layout taken from the preset's lines, never from a fixed width (Alimardon, 2026-10-08: phones later, so nothing built now needs rework, M9; the Tablet and Phone presets bring their own panels).
- Colours and sizes come only from the design tokens (`edel::tokens`); never hard-code one in drawing code.
- Redraw only what changed, and only when it changed: an idle panel draws nothing between minutes. Draw a surface at most once per frame: after each drawing it waits for the compositor's frame callback, then draws what shows by then, so its shared buffers stay two however fast things change.
- Every word people read, shown or spoken to a screen reader, is marked with `edel::i18n`'s `tr`, `trf` or `n_` (M5.24a), and `po/shell-ui.pot` is written afresh with `EDEL_WRITE_DOCS=1 cargo test -p edel i18n` in the same commit; log lines stay English.

## Layout

- `main.rs`: the Wayland connection; the tokens in the colour scheme `appearance.mode` names (M5.5c, `edel::tokens::load`); the preset (`layout.preset` from the machine's and the person's settings file through `edel::settings`, else Classic, read once at start with the colour scheme by `from_system_files` through `edel::presets`, its panels replaced by `layout.panels` when set, M5.4e) and one layer surface per panel in it (namespace `edel-panel`, along its edge, exclusive zone `size.panel`, a `size.radius` strip on its inner side for the fillets that is neither opaque nor clickable; a dock (M5.4d, `style = "dock"`) is namespace `edel-dock`, anchored to its edge alone 8 px from it, keeps `size.panel` free too unless it hides while covered (`hide = "covered"`, M5.4f, which the compositor does), and asks for a new width from `paint::natural_width` whenever what it holds changes, drawing once the compositor agrees); the calloop loop with the minute timer that redraws the panels; buffers from a `SlotPool`; each panel's scale from `preferred_buffer_scale`; the tier from the compositor's state file (fillets unless Lite); a pointer from the seat, whose left clicks and scrolls go to the widget under them (`Shell::input`, by the places `paint` returned), and the `Live` state widgets show, kept by `workspaces_changed` and `windows_changed`; scrolls add up in `widgets::Scrolled` (a high-resolution wheel's 120ths, a touchpad's pixels) until they make whole steps; the launcher's surface (`Menu`: a `Popup` named `edel-launcher`, anchored beside the first panel's start, 8 px from the panel and the side, with the keyboard alone) and the seat's keyboard (sctk's xkbcommon, with repeat), all made in `open_launcher` and let go in `close_launcher`, which the keyboard leaving also calls; keys go to `launcher_key`, the pointer's motion and clicks on it to its rows; it logs `launcher shown, N apps`, `launched NAME (COMMAND)` and `launcher hidden`; each change of where widgets lie is logged as `panel places NAME X+W, ...` in logical pixels, which CI reads to click them. It exits when the compositor goes away or takes a panel away; the compositor starts it again (`shellui.rs` there).
- `paint.rs`: `Look` (everything a panel shows at one scale, what each widget shows included), `Row` (the panel's widgets, from its start, in its centre and towards its end) and `paint`, which draws a panel into a tiny-skia pixmap (its colour, the fillets on either edge and the widgets laid out in their groups; for a dock a card `DOCK_HEIGHT` (60 px) high, rounded all round at 18 px with a hairline of the text's colour at 10 percent inside its edge, 8 px at each end (`DOCK_PAD`), and no fillets; `height` is a panel's height by style) and returns where each widget lies; `natural_width` is how wide a dock is for what it shows; `strip` and `panel_top` say where the panel lies in its buffer for each style; `Text` (cosmic-text's fonts and glyph cache, loaded once, in the tokens' `font.interface` family) shapes and draws a `Line`, and `fit` cuts one short with an ellipsis; `mix` dims a colour towards another; `lit` is the accent at 18%, under a chosen row or a switched-on button; `rounded` is a rounded rectangle path and `fill` fills one; `to_argb` gives `wl_shm`'s byte order. Tested without a display. `shadow` (M5.5e) draws a card's shadow from the tokens: two layers cast by one light above, a close sharp one and a far soft one, each a rounded-rect mask blurred by three box blurs (`box_blur`), in the tokens' `shadow` colour.
- `widgets/mod.rs` (M5.1c): the widget table. A widget is one module here and one line in `TABLE`: its name (what presets write), the feature it needs if any, and its functions: what it shows now, from `Live` (the panel redraws only when that changes), its width and how it draws, on a `Canvas`, both from what it shows, and what an `Input` (a click with the widget's width, a scroll) on it does, an `Action` (show a workspace, move the switcher's view, activate or minimize a window) or nothing (`no_input`); and its `role` and `label` for screen readers (M5.1d), with `parts` (`no_parts` for most) when a screen reader should read its pieces one by one, as the tray's icons (M5.2e). `usable` keeps the widgets a preset names that this machine can show and notes the rest: a name the table lacks, or a widget whose feature has no `/usr/share/edel/features/NAME.toml`. The tests fail when a line needs a feature with no file under `features/` or a built-in preset names a widget the table lacks.
- `a11y.rs` (M5.1d): what screen readers read. `tree` makes each panel a window in an AccessKit tree whose children are its widgets of some width, with the role and label from each widget's line (a label's text is its value) and their places from `paint`; a `Reader` per panel holds an `accesskit_unix::Adapter`, which serves the tree over AT-SPI on the session's D-Bus only once a reader turns accessibility on (`org.a11y.Status`). Until then `update` keeps the panel's widgets and builds no tree. Widgets take no actions from a reader yet. A widget's `parts` (M5.2e) become the children of its node. `ci/desktop/features/desktop-test/usr/libexec/edel-a11y-tree` is the test's screen reader. Tested.
- `tray.rs` (M5.2e): the tray's D-Bus side, on the connection and zbus thread of the portal, with no thread of its own. `serve` puts `org.kde.StatusNotifierWatcher` at `/StatusNotifierWatcher` there (`RegisterStatusNotifierItem`, `RegisterStatusNotifierHost` doing nothing, `RegisteredStatusNotifierItems`, `IsStatusNotifierHostRegistered` always true, `ProtocolVersion` 0 and the three signals) and claims the name; if another tray host has it, it says so (`messages::tray_not_served`) and there is no tray. An item is known by its id, `service/path` (`id_of`: a bus name alone is at `/StatusNotifierItem`, an object path is on the sender, both are as given; `parse_id` splits it). Each registered item gets a task on zbus's executor (`watch`): it listens to the item's signals and to its bus name losing its owner (`NameOwnerChanged`), reads `IconName`, `IconPixmap`, `Title` and `ToolTip` fresh (no property cache) at the start and after any signal, and sends `Event::Item` or, when the app leaves the bus, `Event::Gone`, over a calloop channel to the event loop, where `Shell::tray_changed` keeps `Live::tray` and logs `tray: N items` when their number changes. The pixmap (ARGB32 big-endian, the best size by `best_pixmap`, premultiplied by `premultiplied`, shrunk to at most 44 px by `shrunk`) travels as the text `encode` writes, so `shows` stays text. `call` asks an item to `Activate` or `ContextMenu` on the same executor without waiting. The test `an_item_registers_shows_up_and_leaves_with_its_app` runs only with a session bus (`dbus-run-session -- cargo test -p edel-shell-ui tray`) and passes without one. Menus over `com.canonical.dbusmenu`, `IconThemePath`, attention and overlay icons and `Status` are not read yet.
- `widgets/tray.rs` (M5.2e): the tray widget, before the clock in every built-in preset. It shows a line per item, `ID\tTITLE\tICON NAME\tPIXMAP`; a 30 px cell each between 2 px margins, the icon 22 px: the item's `IconName` from the icon themes as apps' are, else its pixmap fitted into the square, else the first letter of its title on an accent tile. No width without items. A click is `Action::Tray(ID, false)` and a right click `Action::Tray(ID, true)`, which `Shell::tray_call` turns into `Activate` or `ContextMenu` with the click's place along the panel (the screen's place is not known to shell-ui). A screen reader hears a group, "Tray: Network, Volume", whose parts are one button for each item named by its title (`a11y::Item::children`). Tested.
- `widgets/menu.rs`: the menu button's icon, `design/icons/menu.svg` (four rounded squares, M5.5d), in a square as tall as the panel; a click opens or closes the launcher (M5.3b).
- `popup.rs` (M5.3): what the launcher and the switcher share. `Popup` is a surface of its own on the overlay layer with its own `SlotPool`, made when shown and dropped when hidden: `canvas` gives a pixmap only when configured and what it shows changed, `show` attaches it, `resize` asks for a new size. `card` draws the menus' card (the panel's colour, `size.radius` corners) and `rows` a list of labels `size.row` high, the chosen one `lit` with `size.radius_control` corners; `radio` draws a radio button. A change to those tokens reaches both. Tested. On the Full and Balanced tiers a popup has `room` round its card (M5.5e, `paint::shadow_room`, the tokens' `shadow_blur` plus `shadow_offset`, none on Lite): the surface is that much bigger, takes clicks only on the card, the launcher's margin shrinks by it so the card keeps its place, pointer positions are taken from the card's corner, and `show` lays the card over `paint::shadow`, drawn once per card size and scale.
- `launcher.rs` (M5.3b): the launcher's look and state. `paint` draws a `View` (what was typed, the matching names, the chosen row) at `size`: a `popup::card`, a search line with the caret in the accent, eight `popup::rows`; `Launcher` reads the apps on `open`, forgets them on `close`, searches as people type, steps the choice and `start`s an app with its stdio closed in the home directory, keeping the child until `reap` (on each opening, closing and minute) collects it. Tested.
- `styles.rs` (M5.16b): the layout button's right-click menu. `styles()` are `layout.tiling_style`'s values from `edel::settings::choices`, so a new style is a new row; `paint` draws a `WIDTH` (200 px) `popup::card` with a row per style, its radio button (`popup::radio`, the panel text's size across, a ring, and on the one `in_use`, the person's file over the machine's, filled with a dot, all in `colour.accent`; the `lit` row's light is only where the pointer or the arrows are), its picture and its name; `main.rs` makes its surface (a `Popup` named `edel-styles`, centred on the button, with the keyboard) on `Action::Styles`, which the layout widget gives for `Input::Menu`, a right click; a click, or Up, Down and Return, writes the key with `edel::settings::write` (taken out when it is what applies without it), logging `styles menu shown, STYLE in use`, `tiling style STYLE chosen` and `styles menu hidden`. Tested.
- `switcher.rs` (M5.3c): the window switcher's picture. `View` is the compositor's titles and chosen place; `paint` draws a `WIDTH` (420 px) `popup::card` whose `size` follows the titles, at most `MOST` (10), as `popup::rows`. `main.rs` makes its surface (a `Popup` named `edel-switcher` with no anchor, so in the middle, and no keyboard) on the first `switcher` event, sizes it again when the number of titles changes and lets it all go on `switcher_hide`, logging `switcher shown, N windows` and `switcher hidden`. Tested.
- `edel::apps` and `edel::app_icons` (in the `edel` library since M5.6a, as the compositor shows apps' icons in title bars too; written once, ADR-010): the `.desktop` files and the icon themes, which the launcher and the apps widget read; see `crates/edel/CLAUDE.md`.
- `widgets/workspaces.rs` (M5.2c): the workspace switcher. It shows `FIRST;NAME,NAME*,...`: at most three round buttons (`SHOWN`), the shown workspace a wider pill in `colour.accent`, the others filled with the panel text's colour at 14 percent, and at a side where more lie the next button peeking in, 14 px of it (`PEEK`), fading out towards the end, as the mockups draw it (Alimardon found the earlier dot plain); its width stays the same as the shown workspace changes. A click shows the workspace under it, a click on a peek or a scroll moves the view, kept in sight. A screen reader hears "Workspaces: 2 shown, 2 of 4". Tested.
- `widgets/layout.rs` (M5.3a): the layout toggle. It shows the shown workspace's policy; one 30 by 26 px button, two overlapping windows while floating, a screen split in three on an accent fill while tiling, its icon `design/icons/layout-floating.svg` or `layout-tiling.svg`, on the mockups' 24-unit square (M5.5d), drawn by `paint::icon`; a click asks `link` to switch. Tested.
- `messages.rs` (M5.28b): what shell-ui says when something fails, one function or constant per message in the words of `docs/MESSAGES.md`, with tests holding the text; routine status lines that CI greps (`launcher shown`, `panel places`) stay where they are written.
- `link.rs` (M5.3a): the client of `edel-shell-v1`, the compositor's private protocol, generated by `wayland-scanner` from `../compositor/protocols/edel-shell-v1.xml`: the policy event into `Live::policy` and `toggle_policy`.
- `widgets/windows.rs` (M5.2h): the window list. It shows a line per window on a screen, `*TITLE` for the focused one, `-TITLE` for a minimized one, ` TITLE` else; every button is one width (`button_width`: at most 180 px, together at most 45% of the panel), its title cut with an ellipsis; when even the narrowest, 32 px, do not fit, `buttons` gives as many as do, the last counting the windows left over (`+N`) and bringing the first of them forward; the focused one is lit with a 14 px accent line along its foot, others have a dot, a minimized one dimmer text and no mark. A click on the focused window minimizes it, on another activates it. A screen reader hears "Windows: Files (focused), Mail (minimized)". Tested.
- `toplevels.rs` (M5.2h): the wlr-foreign-toplevel-management client, through `Dispatch2` user data (`Manager`, `Handle`): each window's title, screens, focus and minimized state; `tasks` gives the windows on a screen, in order, which `activate` (with the seat) and `minimize` address by place; `windows_changed` at each `done` and `closed`; without the protocol the list shows nothing.
- `workspaces.rs` (M5.2c): the ext-workspace-v1 client, through sctk's `Dispatch2` user data (`Manager`, `Group`, `Handle`): the workspaces by name with the shown one marked, `show` (activate, then commit), and `workspaces_changed` at each `done`; a workspace or group removed is destroyed; without the protocol the switcher shows nothing.
- `widgets/clock.rs`: the clock, `HH:MM` with room on both sides, and the time until the next minute. Tested.
- `widgets/title.rs` (M5.4a): the focused window's title, for bars along the top such as Hive's: the panel text's colour and size, cut short at 40% of the panel, no width while no window has the keyboard. Tested.
- `widgets/apps.rs` (M5.4c): the apps, as a taskbar shows them. It shows a line per cell, `STATE\tID\tICON\tNAME` (`*` focused, `+` running, a space for a pinned app not running): the preset's pinned apps (`Live::pinned`, read once at start, only when a panel holds the widget), then every other app with a window open, named and drawn from `Live::installed` or from its app id. A 40 px cell each, the icon 24 px, the focused one lit with the window list's accent line, a running one with its dot, an app without an icon its initial on an accent tile; in a dock (`Canvas::dock`) the cell is the dock's height less 8 px and the icon 40 px, with a round dot under it, the accent for the focused app. `belongs` matches a window's app id to a desktop file id, or its last part. A click is `Action::App(ID)`: `main.rs` minimizes the app's focused window, else brings its first window forward, else starts it through `Launcher::run`. Tested.
- `widgets/search.rs` (M5.4c): a 180 by 30 px pill with a magnifier and "Search"; a click opens or closes the launcher. Tested.
- `portal.rs` (M5.5a): the settings portal's backend. `serve` claims `org.freedesktop.impl.portal.desktop.edel` on the session's bus and serves `org.freedesktop.impl.portal.Settings` at `/org/freedesktop/portal/desktop` on zbus's own thread (the connection lives in `Shell`); `settings` is `org.freedesktop.appearance` from the tokens: `color-scheme` 1 when the background is dark, else 2, `accent-color` the accent, `contrast` 0. Once serving, it emits `SettingChanged` for each (M5.5c), as shell-ui starts again when the colour scheme changes, so apps already open follow. xdg-desktop-portal, which D-Bus starts when an app first asks, passes it on, by `/usr/share/xdg-desktop-portal/portals/edel.portal` and `edel-portals.conf` in the shell feature. Without a session bus it says so and serves nothing. Tested.
- `features/NAME.rs` (none yet): code that talks to an OS feature, such as NetworkManager for `network` (M5.9); its widget's line says `needs: Some("NAME")`.

## Running it

Inside a running compositor: `WAYLAND_DISPLAY=wayland-N cargo run -p edel-shell-ui`. The compositor starts it by itself when `edel-shell-ui` is on the `PATH`, as in the desktop image (the `shell` feature).
