# shell-ui

`edel-shell-ui`: the Edel shell's panels and menus (ADR-002), the second of its two long-running processes. Edition 2024, `rust-version = "1.85"`, GPL-3.0-or-later, `publish = false`.

## What belongs here

- What people use to find and switch things: the panel (M5.1b), its workspace switcher (M5.2c), window list (M5.2h), layout toggle (M5.3a), apps and search field (M5.4c), the launcher (M5.3b) and the window switcher's list (M5.3c), the tray (M5.2e), the status area and quick settings (M5.9a), notifications with the calendar and do not disturb (M5.9b) and the lock screen and greeter (M5.10). Windows, title bars and effects belong to the compositor; settings pages to Settings.
- It draws its own surfaces (ADR-002's shell-ui toolkit decision of 2026-10-03): layer-shell surfaces through smithay-client-toolkit, drawing with tiny-skia into shared memory, text shaped by cosmic-text. No GTK, no GLib, no scripting, no theme engine. [docs/REVIEW-shells.md](../../docs/REVIEW-shells.md) is what its look and motion learn from.
- Every surface works at every size class (M5.6c) and in touch mode: a panel, dock, launcher or switcher drawn on a Compact screen (below 600 logical px) fits it and gives touch targets of at least 44 px, its layout taken from the preset's lines, never from a fixed width (Alimardon, 2026-10-08: phones later, so nothing built now needs rework, M9; the Tablet and Phone presets bring their own panels).
- Colours and sizes come only from the design tokens (`edel::tokens`); never hard-code one in drawing code. The panel's own (M5.29, from the mockups): `size.panel` (40, the bar), `panel_control` (30, a button's height), `panel_icon` (20, an app's icon in a button), `panel_glyph` (18, the shell's own icons), `panel_text` (13) and `panel_text_small` (11, the date and the workspaces' digits); the paddings between them are consts in each widget.
- Redraw only what changed, and only when it changed: an idle panel draws nothing between minutes. Draw a surface at most once per frame: after each drawing it waits for the compositor's frame callback, then draws what shows by then, so its shared buffers stay two however fast things change.
- **Behind a game nothing ticks (M7.7c):** while the compositor's `playing` event says a game plays on every screen, `Shell::playing` is true and shell-ui does no timed work. Anything that redraws or reads with the minute goes in `Shell::minute` (`main.rs`), which the tick skips then and runs once when the game ends (`aside.rs`); a new timer of its own checks `self.playing` and waits, as the update check does. Work that answers an event (a click, a notification, UPower) goes on as before. What a game itself needs (full power on the charger, M7.7b) follows `Shell::game`, a game on some screen (the `game` event), never `playing`, which waits for every screen so a second screen's clock goes on.
- Every word people read, shown or spoken to a screen reader, is marked with `edel::i18n`'s `tr`, `trf` or `n_` (M5.24a), and `po/shell-ui.pot` is written afresh with `EDEL_WRITE_DOCS=1 cargo test -p edel i18n` in the same commit; log lines stay English.
- **One copy of everything (ADR-010, Alimardon, 2026-10-10):** what two surfaces show is one module both call, never a second copy: the notification card (`notice.rs`), the slider (`quick::slider`), the cards and rows (`popup.rs`), and from M5.10c the player (`player.rs`). Before drawing something, look for the module that already draws it; when a second surface needs a part another surface owns, move the part into its own module first, in the same PR. Each part takes its size and colours from the tokens, so people change it in one place and every surface follows.
- **Stands alone (M8.13):** it needs the `edel` library without its `cli` and `os` features (M6.17: the settings format and its models only), a Wayland compositor with the layer shell, the session bus for the tray, notifications and the settings portal, and the files `edel::places` names. The programs it runs by name are listed in `ci/seams.sh`'s `allowed_program()` with what happens where each is missing (M6.17): `edel`, `edel-settings`, `pw-dump`, `pw-cli` and `pkill`. It never depends on the compositor's code, Settings or the test client, and speaks to the compositor only through Wayland protocols, `edel-shell-v1` among them (`ci/seams.sh`).

## Layout

What each module holds is in [LAYOUT.md](LAYOUT.md), one bullet per module. Read the bullets of the modules you change before editing them, and keep them true in the same PR (a new module adds its bullet there). Rules for the whole crate stay in this file.

## Running it

Inside a running compositor: `WAYLAND_DISPLAY=wayland-N cargo run -p edel-shell-ui`. For a picture to show while coding, `sh ci/look.sh NAME [COMMAND...]` (Docker) runs the compositor and shell-ui inside the release's Alpine, with its fonts and the desktop's feature files, nested under Xvfb at the VM tests' 1280x800, and keeps `out/look/NAME.png`, which matches CI's screenshots; never show one drawn with the host's own fonts and libraries. `EDEL_LOOK_WAIT` (seconds before the picture) and `EDEL_LOOK_FLOAT` (no maximize) reach the container; keys sent with `xdotool` reach the nested compositor only after `xdotool windowfocus` on the window under the pointer, and the nested screen stays 1280x800, so a Settings row near the foot of a long page is out of reach there (CI's tour shows it). Every surface draws with `paint.rs` and the tokens, in the look of the fifth round of mockups (`docs/mockups/shell/`) and of the surfaces already built (the panel, quick settings, the notification centre). The compositor starts it by itself when `edel-shell-ui` is on the `PATH`, as in the desktop image (the `shell` feature).
