# Desktop test cases

What each case of `desktop-test.sh CASE...` checks, one line per case (moved here from [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) by M6.17, line 11), so a pull request that changes a case edits only its own line.

- `background` (M6.2a, first): `background` lists the processes whose names hold `flatpak`, `portal` or `permission-store`; before any app starts there must be none, as Flatpak's helpers and the portals start on D-Bus activation.
- `floating`: the clients at 522,325 and 604,382 in the state file, centred in the 1280x760 area above shell-ui's panel, `two` focused and on top, and their colours at 530,333 and 704,457.
- `titlebar`: every window has `title_bar = true`, the bars' left ends in the tokens' `title_bar` and, for focused `two`, `title_bar_focused`, two's title drawn, a click on two's close button at 791,368 followed by `unmapped window two` and two windows, and a drag of one's bar from 560,311 to 760,131 moving it to 722,145.
- `tiling`: `tiling on` runs `edel settings set layout.tiling=true`, after which the compositor logs `windows now tiling` and the windows line shows foot at 9,36 and one at 645,36 626x715, not overlapping, with a bar colour at each tile's top left; then Super+Y, `windows now floating`, and both back at 440,249 and 722,145.
- `shape` (M5.12b2b, right after `tiling`): `shape on` sets `appearance.gaps=12`, `appearance.border_width=3` and the focused border's two colours to `#ff0000` in the machine's file, which the compositor logs as `shape gaps 12/12, border 3, title bar 28`; then `tiling on` and the windows line gives foot at 15,42 and one at 649,42 616x703, whose frames (the window grown by 3 at the sides and bottom and by 30 on top) are 12 px apart; the pixel halfway between them is neither foot's, one's, the title bars' nor the test client's colour, and one's left border 1 px inside its frame, halfway down, is `ff0000` (`shape.png`); `tiling off` and `shape default` reset the four keys, and the compositor logs `shape gaps 8/8, border 1, title bar 28` again.
